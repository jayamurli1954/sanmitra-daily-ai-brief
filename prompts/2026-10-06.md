### Norway proposes temporary ban on wearable AI glasses in public and educational spaces
Source: Reuters
https://www.reuters.com/technology/norway-propose-temporary-ban-ai-glasses-some-public-places-2026-10-05/

The Norwegian government announced plans to introduce emergency legislation proposing a temporary prohibition on wearable artificial intelligence glasses in sensitive public environments, including schools, beaches, public transit, and healthcare facilities. ### MIT researchers unveil VISTA visual harness enabling perfect ARC-AGI-3 benchmark scores
Source: DeepTech via NetEase
https://www.163.com/dy/article/L8G8MBRJ05119734.html
Also: arXiv https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02200

Researchers at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, including Kaiming He and Cathy Wu, introduced VISTA, an open-source visual harness that grants multimodal artificial intelligence systems lossless visual memory across complex dynamic environments. Without fine-tuning underlying model weights, VISTA enabled Claude Opus 5.0 to advance from a baseline score of forty percent to a perfect one hundred percent on the ARC-AGI-3 reasoning evaluation while requiring fifty-seven percent fewer iterative actions than human baselines. The architecture demonstrates that agentic reasoning bottlenecks often stem from perceptual memory degradation rather than fundamental model capacity limits.
