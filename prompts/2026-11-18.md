### Southeast Asia’s data center boom leaned on Chinese demand. New U.S. rules put that pipeline at risk
Source: CNBC
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-us-ai-chip-export-controls-data-centers-.html

Washington's latest export controls could put the brakes on Southeast Asia's rapid build-out of data centers, CNBC reports. Under an interim rule the Commerce Department issued on Monday, data centers based in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia must now obtain a license from the U.S. government before buying advanced artificial intelligence chips, Nvidia's among them. Operators whose facilities already host large Chinese clients have 90 days to seek that license, or they risk export restrictions down the line. The measure takes aim at the region's growing function as a supplier of computing power to companies from China. Construction of data centers across Southeast Asia has climbed sharply, driven by demand for the semiconductors used to train and build AI models. Because U.S. curbs keep Nvidia's top-tier chips out of China, Chinese tech groups such as Alibaba Group and ByteDance have turned to leasing capacity in data centers abroad.

### Security flaw found in Java, world's third-largest programming language
Source: BBC
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cke89jevqm1vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

A cyber-security start-up has used artificial intelligence to detect a critical weakness in Java, one of the world's most widely used programming languages, according to the BBC. The firm, Calif, located the bug in the way Java handles memory. The company had recently exposed a separate security problem in another language, PHP. Java underpins systems at almost every major global company, including Apple and Amazon, and is also relied on by numerous banks, hospitals and governments. Computer scientist Professor Toby Walsh noted that the language is designed to strengthen security by taking care of memory management. That, he said, is why "this is such an unlikely and serious flaw". The finding follows several significant technology disruptions, among them a large outage at Microsoft. Separately, the US National Security Agency said on Tuesday that its internal communications had gone down, which it attributed to "scheduled routine maintenance".

### TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22115

In a paper titled TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks, the researchers introduce Xing 4, a new family of large language models that builds on the earlier TeleChat series. The models were developed on Ascend domestic chips, and the report explains how the team designed both the models and the computing systems used to train them. The researchers examine trade-offs such as activating only parts of a model at a time and speeding up text generation, weighing model power against computing and memory costs. Training relied on the MindSpore HyperParallel framework to split work efficiently across many machines and handle very long inputs.

Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-clarifies-when-it-will-and-wont-keep-financing-neoclouds/

Nvidia has spelled out when it will continue to fund so-called neoclouds, and the issue matters because of how its ousted chief executive operated, TechCrunch reports. Over the weekend, 197,000 people, Elon Musk among them, signed a petition urging the chipmaker to reinstate Jensen Huang. ### Felicis leads $7.5M bet on Unsiloed AI to make enterprise documents usable by AI agents
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/felicis-leads-7-5m-bet-on-unsiloed-ai-to-make-enterprise-documents-usable-by-ai-agents/

Unsiloed AI has secured $7.5 million in seed funding to help companies make their documents usable by AI agents, TechCrunch reports. The round was led by Silicon Valley venture firm Felicis, joined by Y Combinator, Orange Collective and Uncorrelated Ventures, along with angel investors Aarthi Ramamurthy and Beth Turner. The startup is targeting a growing obstacle for businesses adopting AI agents to automate tasks: much of the information they need sits inside complicated files. Corporate documents frequently contain tables, charts and intricate formatting that AI systems may fail to read correctly. Unsiloed AI says it is building specialized technology to make that material easier for agents to access. Errors in extracting such information can prove expensive, and the consequences could be especially serious for institutions like banks, hospitals and law firms, which depend on these documents in their everyday operations.

### Nvidia promises that revenue will keep soaring — for now
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-promises-that-revenue-will-keep-soaring-for-now/

Nvidia has told investors its run of record revenue will continue, despite upheaval following the exit of founder and chief executive Jensen Huang, TechCrunch reports. On Tuesday, Colette Kress, the former chief financial officer now serving as interim CEO, announced quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion. That was 103% higher than a year earlier and ahead of the company's own forecast of $108 billion for the third quarter of fiscal 2027. Kress guided for a still larger fourth quarter, with revenue projected at $130 billion, give or take 3%. She added that revenue for the full year is set to surpass expectations. Speaking on the earnings call, Kress said: "We are raising our fiscal year 2028 outlook." Nvidia now anticipates revenue growth of about 75% for that year, up from its earlier 70% estimate, measured against a fiscal 2027 base that has itself risen.

### Nvidia is abandoning China after Jensen Huang’s ouster
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-is-abandoning-china-after-jensen-huangs-ouster/

Nvidia will stop selling its H200 chips in China, acting chief executive Colette Kress said on Tuesday, TechCrunch reports. The move ends the company's use of a contested exception to the U.S. ban on exporting advanced chips to the country. China's access to cutting-edge AI accelerators was a key subject on Nvidia's first earnings call since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last week. On the same day, Nvidia said it was forming a board committee to oversee national security policy, export control compliance and global trade risk. The H200 arrangement had been negotiated by Huang and President Donald Trump. It required the chips to pass through the United States so the government could levy a 25% tariff. Critics who favor a hard line on China objected, wanting to keep computing power from Chinese labs and preserve America's AI lead. Huang had consistently argued that competing inside China mattered more.

# INDIA

### Beyond the AI hype: How Industrial AI is reshaping India’s factories and infrastructure
Source: The Economic Times
https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/beyond-the-ai-hype-how-industrial-ai-is-reshaping-indias-factories-and-infrastructure/articleshow/135934331.cms

Artificial intelligence is shifting from chatbots and coding tools into the physical backbone of India's economy, the Economic Times reports. The trend covers AI-driven engineering, digital twins, self-running buildings and smarter railways, where success is judged by productivity, speed of innovation, energy savings and resilience. So-called industrial AI blends artificial intelligence with engineering expertise, live operating data, automation and digital twins, which are virtual replicas of real systems, to improve the running of factories, buildings, power grids and transport networks. This shift is the central theme of Transform, Siemens India Innovation Day 2026, held under the banner "AI for the Real World." The emphasis is on putting AI to work in industrial settings, rather than treating it as a standalone trial, where dependability, safety, cybersecurity and tangible results are essential. For companies, the key question is moving away from AI's capabilities toward where it can produce measurable value.
