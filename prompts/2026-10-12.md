https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2026/oct/11/nuclear-war-and-malicious-use-of-ai-threaten-end-of-humanity-by-2100-says-lancet-assessment

Humans could become extinct by the year 2100 due to malicious use of AI or nuclear conflict, according to a new global risk assessment. They top a list of 17 major threats to human health over the next 75 years, which also includes cuts to aid spending by wealthy countries, the climate crisis and childhood malnutrition. ### Trump administration leans on liability law to police AI models that go rogue
Source: The Japan Times (Bloomberg)
https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/10/11/tech/trump-ai-liability-models-rogue/

Washington's AI safety plan is shaping up as report it, or be liable for it. A Bloomberg analysis published over the weekend lays out how the Trump administration is leaning on existing liability law instead of new rules. The White House's new Super Intelligence Force, led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, warned developers after Anthropic disclosed that its models had acted on real websites that companies must immediately disclose incidents involving their models, and that delayed notification will not be tolerated. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and former AI czar David Sacks back making developers pay for harm, and the FTC is already questioning OpenAI, Anthropic and others about product safety. The catch, legal experts say, is that the main US anti-hacking law requires intent, which an AI agent does not have. Senators Josh Hawley and Chris Murphy plan a bill to make developers liable for weak safeguards on hacking abilities, and Representative Lori Trahan has drafted one that holds them responsible even when they took reasonable care.

### Context Language Models let AI agents rewrite their own working memory
Source: MPost
https://mpost.io/meta-presents-context-language-models-ai-agents-that-edit-their-own-memory-outperform-fixed-harnesses-at-lower-compute-cost/
Also: alphaXiv https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.37725

What if a model decided for itself what to remember? Researchers from Meta, MIT and the University of Washington describe Context Language Models, which treat their working memory as a file they can freely rewrite, keeping key facts, deleting dead ends and writing their own progress notes, instead of relying on fixed summarising or retrieval rules. With no extra training, they reported 11.4 percent higher accuracy with 21.5 percent less compute on the BrowseComp-Plus research benchmark, and 5 percent better scores with 59 percent less compute on a 12-hour task suite. Reinforcement learning lifted Qwen3.5-9B on BrowseComp-Plus from 28.8 percent to 42.5 percent. The authors flag real risks: a model can throw away something it later needs, and an editable memory gives prompt injections a place to hide.

# INDIA

### A quarter of India's workforce is highly exposed to AI, says study launched by Chief Economic Adviser
Source: Whalesbook
https://whalesbook.com/news/English/economy/25percent-of-Indias-Workforce-Faces-High-AI-Exposure-Study/6acba7ac79a16deaeaed7119

About a quarter of India's workforce is now highly exposed to AI, according to a white paper from the Great Lakes Institute of Management's Centre for Finance and Economic Research in Chennai, launched by Chief Economic Adviser V Anantha Nageswaran on Sunday. The share of high-routine occupations has grown since 2012. Exposure is not the same as job loss: 7.6 percent of workers are positioned to gain productivity as AI complements their work, while 11.4 percent face potential displacement. Agriculture and skilled trades, which employ over 54 percent of the working population, show low substitution risk. The risk is sharpest for the young: 39.4 percent of workers aged 15 to 24 are in roles with high substitution risk, and for them high-routine jobs outnumber complementary roles by 7.6 to 1, against 4 to 1 for older workers. The Chief Economic Adviser warned that when AI agents can do routine work cheaply, training junior employees becomes a cost for firms, and the authors call for large-scale reskilling.
