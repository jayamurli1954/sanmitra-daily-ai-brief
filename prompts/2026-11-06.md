### Russia and US seek to block plan for binding rules on autonomous weapons
Lead: yes
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/06/autonomous-weapons-ai-russia-us-binding-rules

UN conference this month seen as key moment after years of discussions on filling gap in governance of AI systems Plans to fill a gap in the global governance of autonomous weapons are in jeopardy as a group of nations led by Russia and the US move to use a UN conference this month to block proposals to negotiate a legally binding set of rules.
