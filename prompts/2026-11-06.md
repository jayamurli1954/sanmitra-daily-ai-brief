Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/06/autonomous-weapons-ai-russia-us-binding-rules

Russia and the United States are leading a bloc of nations seeking to derail negotiations on legally binding regulation of autonomous weapons, according to the Guardian. ### VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08449

The researchers behind a paper titled VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding are tackling a speed bottleneck in AI used for robots and self-driving cars. Such systems can plan not just in language but visually, marking their goals and forecasting motion with points, boxes around objects and paths. Today's vision-language models, which combine image understanding with text, generate those plans one coordinate after another, so richer plans are slower to produce. VFlash exploits the regular shape of these visual elements to compute all their coordinates simultaneously, using a compact helper model that drafts a whole element in one step.

# INDIA

### India's CERT-In asks organisations to audit what their AI agents can access and do
Lead: yes
CERT-In, India's national cybersecurity agency, is urging organisations to check whether their AI agents could leak confidential data or take actions that were never authorised, The Times of India reports. The agency's guidelines on auditing AI security call for strict permission controls, a log of every step an agent carries out, an emergency mechanism to halt an agent, and human sign-off before any significant action. They also address prompt injection, in which harmful instructions hidden within emails, documents or web pages attempt to hijack an agent. Indian companies are beginning to let AI agents into their financial records, internal systems, email and documents. The guidance treats permissions for agents and audit trails as a fundamental security measure, rather than something considered later.
