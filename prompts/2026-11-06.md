### Russia and US seek to block plan for binding rules on autonomous weapons
Lead: yes
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/06/autonomous-weapons-ai-russia-us-binding-rules

A group of countries headed by Russia and the United States is working to stop talks on legally binding rules for autonomous weapons, the Guardian reports. They plan to use a United Nations conference this month to block the proposals. Many see the meeting as a turning point after years of debate over how to govern weapons systems run by artificial intelligence. Efforts to close that gap in oversight now appear to be at risk. ### VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08449

In a paper titled VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding, the researchers take on a speed problem in AI systems used for robotics and self-driving cars. These systems do not have to plan only in words. They can also plan visually, using points, boxes drawn around objects, and paths to mark their goals and predict movement. But current models that combine vision and language build these plans one coordinate at a time, so more detailed plans take longer to produce. VFlash takes advantage of the predictable shape of these visual elements to work out all of their coordinates at once. It relies on a small helper model that drafts an entire element in a single step.
