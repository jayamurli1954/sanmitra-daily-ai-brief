### Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12806

Robots that handle objects with their hands depend heavily on a sense of touch, but today's commercial touch sensors are still immature, and each type has its own strengths and weaknesses. In a paper titled Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation, the researchers present LingBot-Touch, a large foundation model for touch. It produces a single shared representation from pressure-based, magnetic and camera-based touch sensors, and can be extended to new sensor types. Its output plugs directly into different robot control systems, including vision-language-action models. Tests in simulation and on several real robots showed it substantially improved complex handling tasks while remaining computationally efficient.

### Australia to introduce mandatory reporting after OpenAI hack to combat artificial intelligence ‘going rogue’
Lead: yes
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/10/australia-manadatory-reporting-openai-medicare-hack

OpenAI will not face criminal penalties over a breach of Medicare data in Australia, but Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has signalled changes to criminal law aimed at future breaches by artificial intelligence companies, the Guardian reports. The government says the changes are meant to stop firms from claiming in future that they were unaware a computer was loaded. On Tuesday, Albanese published the recommendations of a rapid review into the June breach of a Medicare statistics reporting portal run by Services Australia. He did not release the review's findings or the full report, but committed to doing so at a later date. Among the measures outlined is keeping security controls in place throughout every stage of development and deployment.

# CHINA

### Ant Group’s Robbyant open-sources tactile AI model for robot hands
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/10/ant-groups-robbyant-open-sources-tactile-ai-model-for-robot-hands/

Robbyant, the robotics arm of Ant Group, has made the model weights and inference code for LingBot-Touch publicly available, TechNode reports. The model is built to help robot hands use touch when handling objects. It was trained on almost 5,000 hours of recordings from three kinds of touch sensors, so it can operate across different hardware. Robbyant tested it on 26 real-world tasks with two robotic hands, including handling fragile items and coordinating both hands.
