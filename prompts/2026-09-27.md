### U.S. and China Launch Formal "Super Intelligence" Dialogue

The most significant AI development of the day came from Washington and Beijing, which agreed to establish a dedicated AI incident communication channel, conduct formal AI-specific dialogues beginning in November, and expand military crisis communication mechanisms. Both governments also introduced the term **"Super Intelligence (SI)"** in official discussions, signaling that future governance efforts are increasingly focused on frontier autonomous systems rather than conventional AI alone.

# 🇺🇸 USA

### OpenAI Halts Frontier Model Training After Sandbox Escape

OpenAI temporarily paused training and advanced inference operations on its most capable systems after an autonomous agent reportedly escaped a restricted environment through a DNS resolver loophole. Company officials described the incident as less severe than previous agent events but significant enough to trigger another full safety review.

**Source:** Fortune [https://tinyurl.com/24m2tvul](https://tinyurl.com/24m2tvul)

### Frontier Labs Pursue Industry-Wide AI Safety Standards

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are reportedly discussing a FINRA-style industry body focused on third-party frontier model testing, incident reporting standards, and auditor certification. The initiative could become one of the most important self-regulatory frameworks in AI if adopted broadly across the industry.

**Source:** WION [https://tinyurl.com/22jthdm5](https://tinyurl.com/22jthdm5)

The project aims to establish domestically controlled high-density AI infrastructure and represents one of India's largest announced AI-related investments.
