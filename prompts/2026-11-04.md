### Binance says fake job interviews used as part of $6.3bn crypto hack
Lead: yes
Source: BBC
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck5ynwz9gy67o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

Hackers who stole a record $6.3bn (£4.7bn) from Binance used fake job interviews and a compromised internal AI tool, the firm has revealed. Initial findings published by the company, which operates the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, said hackers gained access to its systems through "sophisticated, multi-stage campaign". It said they used a range of techniques such as malicious software downloads to compromise the computers of Binance engineers and gain control over tools to drain its wallet of billions of dollars worth of crypto. ### AI inference chipmaker Moffett AI raises nearly RMB 1 billion in Series C+ funding
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/03/ai-inference-chipmaker-moffett-ai-raises-nearly-rmb-1-billion-in-series-c-funding/

Moffett AI, a Shenzhen-based developer of AI inference chips, announced on November 3 that it had completed nearly RMB 1 billion in Series C+ funding. The financing follows another round of nearly RMB 1 billion approximately five months earlier. The proceeds will support upgrades to its sparse-computing inference chips, research into next-generation processors, and collaboration with research institutions and industry partners. Sparse computing reduces the calculations required to run AI models by exploiting sparsity in their data and operations. Moffett AI said it has deployed inference clusters across four regions in China and partnered with telecommunications operators. Its next-generation SP200 accelerator card is scheduled to arrive before the end of the year.
