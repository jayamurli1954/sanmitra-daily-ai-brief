### Meta launches Muse in UK – the AI agent that can do your Christmas shopping
Source: The Guardian
https://is.gd/d9gUHw

Meta is bringing its Muse AI assistant to the UK, pitching it as a way for shoppers to avoid the Christmas crowds, the Guardian reports. ### Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29897

The researchers behind a paper titled Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control are tackling how to generate long, controllable movement in real time, something animated characters and humanoid robots need when instructions and destinations change mid-motion. Current diffusion methods that build movement step by step struggle to recall events from far back, and their repeated noise-removal passes slow them down. The team's system, built on the Mamba architecture, combines short-range attention, recurrent states that can be resumed, and a feature called persistent content memory, which turns compressed motion history and text cues into continually updated slots the model can draw on.

### OpenAI and Cisco launch Shirudo, an open-source tool to shield Linux systems from exploits
Source: Analytics India Magazine
https://tinyurl.com/26agp8yp

OpenAI and Cisco have released Shirudo, an open-source security tool that protects running Linux systems from known weaknesses without a software update or a restart, Analytics India Magazine reports. It relies on a Linux technology called eBPF to watch what a system is doing and block specific malicious behaviour. The protection comes as small programs called shields, each aimed at a vulnerable part of a piece of software, so the application can stay online. In a demonstration, a shield written with OpenAI's Codex stopped a Java4Shell exploit aimed at a live Elasticsearch service: malicious input was rejected while the service kept running. OpenAI president Greg Brockman said that finding a bug today does not mean every system can be patched tomorrow, and that the tool is meant to protect systems while teams build and roll out a permanent fix.

### Google Search’s AI Mode can now tap into Drive and Wallet for more personalized responses
Source: TechCrunch
https://is.gd/8WDESM

Google said on Monday it is extending Personal Intelligence, a feature within AI Mode in Search, to Google Drive, Google Wallet and the Gemini app, TechCrunch reports. Personal Intelligence aims to give users answers tailored to their circumstances by linking with their Google apps and drawing on details from their online lives. It already works with Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Photos. # ASIA

### Kate Bush and Grace Jones urge Australian government not to legalise AI ‘theft’
Source: The Guardian
https://tinyurl.com/2ak6a3mk

Kate Bush, Grace Jones and Sir Ian Rankin are among 7,000 artists and creative workers worldwide urging the Australian government not to weaken copyright law, the Guardian reports. Their appeal asks Labor not to allow the work of the world's creators to be legally taken on Australian soil. The campaign is the latest in a global backlash against artificial intelligence companies seeking looser copyright rules. It came on the same day a long-awaited parliamentary committee released a lengthy report recommending copyright reform in Australia. Labor MP Jo Briskey, who chairs the committee, told the paper that existing copyright laws stop frontier AI models from training on Australian content. Annabelle Herd, chief executive of the recording industry association Aria, said any attempt to dilute those protections would harm creatives.

### Cashfree launches a wallet that lets AI agents pay on behalf of Indian users
Source: Analytics India Magazine
https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/cashfree-launches-wallet-letting-ai-agents-pay-for-indian-users

Payments company Cashfree has launched Cashfree Wallet, which lets people authorise their personal AI agents to make payments for them, Analytics India Magazine reports. Users decide the budget an agent can spend and the rules it must follow. For now agents can use the wallet only to pay bills, but Cashfree plans to add travel, food, groceries and online shopping. Users link the wallet to an agent by confirming their mobile number with a one-time password, top it up through UPI, card or net banking, and set spending limits. Agents pay merchants on Cashfree's checkout only from the wallet, so they never see the user's bank account or card details, and all payment data is stored in India. The report cites a McKinsey finding that India ranks second in the world for AI-powered shopping.
