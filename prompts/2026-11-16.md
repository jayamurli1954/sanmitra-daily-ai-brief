# WORLD

### Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18359

A new research paper, titled Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems, argues that robotics has entered a different phase. The researchers say the arrival of GPT-6 Astra kicked off what they call agentic robotics. Within a few weeks, large frontier models wrapped in agent software equalled or beat specialised robot controllers on many physical-world tests. Rather than training a separate system for each group of tasks, one foundation model handles open-ended jobs in a continuous cycle. It watches its surroundings, works out what the user wants, plans, uses skills and tools, checks results and adjusts. The researchers note the field still lacks an agreed definition, a dependable way to measure progress and a plan for future research.
