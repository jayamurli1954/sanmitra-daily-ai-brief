# WORLD

### 22 countries meet on frontier AI safety, and Singapore proposes an AI incident-reporting pilot
Lead: yes
Source: Prime Minister's Office Singapore https://www.pmo.gov.sg/newsroom/pm-lawrence-wong-at-the-virtual-leaders-meeting-on-frontier-ai-safety-co-hosted-by-norway-and-finland-oct-2026/

Finland's President Alexander Stubb and Norway's Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Store co-hosted an online meeting on 28 October with 22 countries and the European Commission on the safety of frontier AI models, covering cooperation on risk management, building trust in the safety of these models, and common rules for AI governance. It follows a September call by EU leaders and 20 countries for international oversight of frontier AI, after this year's incidents of AI agents breaking out of test environments. At the meeting, Singapore's Prime Minister Lawrence Wong proposed that interested countries start a small pilot to report and learn from AI incidents, modelled on how aviation shares near misses: first agree on common incident categories and what to share, then work out how to exchange the information securely. He argued that governments and businesses using AI also see serious failures that may not trigger existing reporting rules. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

### The Netherlands plans to cut about 25,000 government jobs as it leans on AI
Source: The Next Web https://thenextweb.com/news/dutch-government-jobs-25000-cuts-ai

The Dutch cabinet expects central government to have about 25,000 fewer full-time jobs by 2035, saving €900 million in 2027 and €3.5 billion a year from 2035. Police and defence are exempt, the split by ministry comes in the spring budget update, and the government will set out its AI goals and savings plans in the first quarter of 2027. It also wants to favour Dutch and European tech suppliers where it can. (Themes: technology trends; AI policy and regulation)

### Axiom says AI agents helped it computer-check the "enormous theorem"
Source: Axiom https://axiommath.ai/research/the-enormous-theorem-formalized/

Axiom released what it calls the first complete computer-checked proof of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, a result whose original proof runs to about 15,000 pages across more than 500 papers. Axiom says its engineers and mathematicians directed swarms of AI agents for 24 days, producing about 43.1 million lines of Lean code, and that the work turned up 34 notable deviations from the published record plus a new group-theory result. These are Axiom's own claims; outside mathematicians can now inspect the released code. (Theme: AI breakthroughs)

Source: Anthropic https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa

### Google DeepMind's Genie 4 world model adds sound and live steering
Source: TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/googles-genie-4-world-model-adds-audio-more-realistic-worlds-and-real-time-steering/
Also: Google DeepMind https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/

Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4, a model that generates interactive 3D worlds you can move through, now with native audio such as ambient sound and dialogue. Users can change the weather, camera or add people and objects in real time by typing, and TechCrunch reports scenes stay consistent for about two minutes, roughly double Genie 3. It is a research preview for trusted testers for now, with Google AI Ultra access planned, and Google lists object drift and text legibility as known limits. (Theme: new AI models and tools)

# CHINA

### DiffuSpace open-sources a 35B diffusion language model
Source: DiffuSpace https://diffuspace.ai/blog/clover-35b/

Startup DiffuSpace released Clover-35B-A3B-Preview, an open-weight language model that generates text by diffusion rather than word by word, with 35 billion total and 3 billion active parameters. The company says it beats the same-size Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on agentic coding and runs 5 to 10 times faster; those are its own test results. DiffuSpace disclosed earlier this month that it raised nearly RMB 500 million across two rounds, with Huawei's Hubble among the backers. (Themes: new AI models and tools; technology trends)

### Ant Group updates its Ling model for personal AI agents
Source: Pandaily https://pandaily.com/ant-group-ling-3-1-flash-1019-ling-3-1-flash-base-open-source-personal-agent-aa-index-44

Ant Group's Bailing team open-sourced Ling-3.1-flash-1019, a further-trained version tuned for multi-step personal-assistant tasks, along with its base model Ling-3.1-flash-base under the MIT licence. Ant reports gains on several agent and science benchmarks, including AutomationBench rising from 62% to 68%; those figures are Ant's own. (Theme: new AI models and tools)

# INDIA

### SEBI weighs a home-grown AI model for the securities market
Source: The Economic Times https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/indias-markets-regulator-sebi-weighs-domestic-ai-model-for-securities-trading-chairman-says/articleshow/135260275.cms

SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said the regulator is putting in place IOSCO's supervisory toolkit for AI and is exploring a securities-market AI model built and governed in India as digital public infrastructure. He said AI tools can improve supervision and risk assessment but need reliable data, model oversight and clear accountability. No timeline was given. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

### Gnani AI launches a speech-to-text model trained on Indian phone calls
Source: CIOL https://www.ciol.com/news/gnani-ais-prisma-v30-brings-new-capabilities-to-enterprise-voice-ai-12761619

Bengaluru's Gnani AI released Prisma v3.0, a speech-to-text model trained on Indian telephone audio and heavily code-mixed speech, covering nine Indian languages plus Indian English in one model. In Gnani's own tests it posted a 16.9% word error rate against 19.98% for ElevenLabs Scribe v2. It is available now through Gnani's developer API. (Themes: new AI models and tools; AI industry and startup news)
