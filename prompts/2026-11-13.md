# WORLD

### Report: UK defence ministry wants targeting that is "autonomous by default"
Lead: yes
Britain's Ministry of Defence is planning a targeting system that official papers describe as "autonomous by default, human by exception," according to the Bureau of Investigative Journalism. The investigative outlet says the documents it obtained also show that an initial version of the ministry's Digital Targeting Web is expected next year. That approach would be a major shift away from the UK's earlier focus on keeping humans in control. The findings reflect the Bureau's own reading of the papers, and the ministry has not confirmed them. The report comes just before United Nations talks on autonomous weapons, which are due to take place in Geneva from 16 to 20 November.
Source: The Bureau of Investigative Journalism https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/stories/2026-11-13/revealed-mod-aiming-to-remove-humans-from-military-targeting-decisions

### SO-Arena: Benchmarking the Capability Spectrum and Reliability of System One Models
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15777

Researchers have released a new testing framework in a paper titled "SO-Arena: Benchmarking the Capability Spectrum and Reliability of System One Models." System One models are designed to make quick, general-purpose decisions without first writing out long streams of text. The researchers argue that overall scores on existing tests say little about how broad these models' skills really are, or how far their choices can be trusted. SO-Arena spans 11 domains and five families of capability. Its correct answers come from reliable sources and are checked by hand. Difficulty is graded using criteria specific to each domain. Models are also tested interactively, taking repeated actions inside sandboxes, which are contained test environments.

### Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; Colette Kress named interim chief
Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive of Nvidia, NPR reports. He stepped down on Thursday, US time, after reports of extramarital affairs. One of them was reportedly with a woman whom US intelligence officials suspect Chinese intelligence had recruited. Huang is also leaving the board. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive while the board considers candidates from both inside and outside Nvidia. Harvey Jones, a director of long standing, becomes the first person ever to chair Nvidia's board. The company says its leadership and product plans will carry on uninterrupted. China has rejected the spying allegations as baseless.

# CHINA

### China Telecom releases a model trained entirely on Huawei Ascend chips
China Telecom has released an AI model trained entirely on Huawei's Ascend chips, TheNextGenTechInsider reports. The model, called Xing4.0-29B-A4B, uses a mixture-of-experts design. It has 29 billion parameters in total, but only 4 billion are used at any one time. China Telecom says it is the first model of this size trained solely on Ascend NPUs using the MindSpore framework, and it reports a score of 75.0 on SWE-bench Verified. Those figures come from the developer. Even so, the release is a real test of China's effort to train AI models without relying on Nvidia.
Source: TheNextGenTechInsider https://www.thenextgentechinsider.com/pulse/china-telecom-launches-xing40-29b-a4b-mixture-of-experts-model

### Chinese AI projects worth over $3 billion depend on restricted Nvidia chips
A Reuters investigation has found that Chinese AI computing projects worth more than 23 billion yuan, or about 3.2 billion dollars, were planned or built around restricted Nvidia processors. These include the H100, H200 and H800 chips. The projects range from data centres to ventures backed by local governments and university infrastructure. Some Chinese AI companies fear the US could further restrict remote use of Nvidia hardware. Several are now looking for computing capacity at data centres abroad, including in Southeast Asia.
Source: Reuters https://www.reuters.com/world/china/huang-exit-us-scrutiny-imperil-3-billion-chinese-ai-projects-reliant-nvidia-2026-11-13/

# INDIA

### MeitY questions platforms that film homes to train robots
The IT ministry, MeitY, met Snabbit, Pronto, Urban Company and several robotics firms on Thursday, The Economic Times reports, citing three sources. The meeting covered gig workers who wear cameras on their heads to film household chores. That footage is used to train physical AI. Officials asked about consent, privacy and how the data is stored. Pronto has already begun filming inside homes, while Urban Company and Snabbit chose not to.
Source: The Economic Times https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/meity-flags-physical-ai-data-use-us-data-centre-woes-may-benefit-india/articleshow/135786144.cms

### CAG to unveil an in-house audit LLM built by IIT Madras
The Comptroller and Auditor General is set to launch CAG LLM 2.0, an in-house AI language model built by IIT Madras, Rediff reports. The model was trained on roughly 65,000 internal inspection reports. It will help auditors with preparatory work and analysis, but it will not conduct audits by itself. The launch is planned for Audit Diwas on 16 November.
