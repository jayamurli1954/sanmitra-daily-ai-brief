# WORLD

### Report: UK defence ministry wants targeting that is "autonomous by default"
The Bureau of Investigative Journalism says official documents it obtained describe a Ministry of Defence targeting system that is "autonomous by default, human by exception", with a first version of its Digital Targeting Web expected next year. That would be a sharp change from the UK's earlier stress on human control. This is based on the Bureau's reading of the documents and has not been confirmed by the ministry. It lands days before UN talks on autonomous weapons in Geneva from 16 to 20 November. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

# USA

### Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; Colette Kress named interim chief
Huang stepped down on Thursday, US time, after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman US intelligence officials reportedly believe was recruited by Chinese intelligence. He also leaves the board. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches inside and outside the company, and longtime director Harvey Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair. Nvidia says its leadership team and product roadmap continue without interruption. China has called the spying allegations baseless. (Theme: AI industry and startup news)

### Nonprofits sue five AI labs to freeze more capable models
A coalition of safety and consumer groups filed suit in San Francisco Superior Court against Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI. They are asking the court to halt training and release of models more capable than today's, and they point to the companies' own warnings about bioweapon and cyberattack risks. These are the plaintiffs' claims. No court has ruled yet. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

### Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast model for coding and agents
Google says Hydrogen brings near-frontier intelligence at lower cost and higher speed, with a one-million-token context window. It is rolling out across the Gemini API, AI Studio, the Gemini app and its Antigravity coding tool, where a new Orchestrator switches between Hydrogen and the larger Carbon model depending on the task. Google Search is also being upgraded with Hydrogen to handle complex, multi-part questions with more structured answers. Speed and quality claims come from Google. (Theme: New AI models and tools)

# CHINA

### China Telecom releases a model trained entirely on Huawei Ascend chips
Xing4.0-29B-A4B is a mixture-of-experts model with 29 billion parameters, of which 4 billion are active at a time. China Telecom says it is the first model of its size trained fully on Ascend NPUs with the MindSpore framework, and it reports a SWE-bench Verified score of 75.0. Those are developer figures, but the release is a real test of China's push to train models without Nvidia. (Theme: AI breakthroughs)

### Chinese AI projects worth over $3 billion depend on restricted Nvidia chips
A Reuters investigation found Chinese AI computing projects worth more than 23 billion yuan, about $3.2 billion, planned or built around restricted Nvidia processors such as the H100, H200 and H800. They include data centres, local-government ventures and university infrastructure. Some Chinese AI firms are now seeking computing capacity at overseas data centres, including in Southeast Asia, fearing tighter US controls on remote access to Nvidia chips. # INDIA

### MeitY questions platforms that film homes to train robots
The IT ministry met Snabbit, Pronto, Urban Company and robotics firms on Thursday about gig workers wearing head cameras to record household tasks for physical-AI training, ET reports, citing three people. Officials asked about consent, privacy and data-storage safeguards. Pronto has started in-home recording, while Urban Company and Snabbit decided against it. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)
