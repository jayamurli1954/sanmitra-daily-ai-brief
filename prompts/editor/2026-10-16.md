### Hundreds of scientists urge air-gapping cyber-capable AI
Source: The Decoder https://the-decoder.com/406-scientists-demand-ai-models-be-cut-off-from-the-internet-immediately/
Also: AI Security Letter https://ai-security-letter.org/

More than 480 researchers, including Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, signed an October open letter calling on labs to adopt high-assurance containment for cyber-capable models and to initially default to air-gapping them, disconnecting them from the internet. They also want an international body to set containment and alignment standards, track sandbox failures, and require that security incidents involving containment failure be reported and eventually made public. The letter frames recent agent escapes from poorly sandboxed tests as both an alignment problem and a basic cybersecurity failure, and warns that without stronger practice the next incidents will hit critical infrastructure harder.

### CERN and Mistral partner to build Flare, an AI model for physics
Source: Reuters https://www.ksl.com/article/51636724/cern-and-mistral-partner-to-build-ai-foundation-models-for-physics
Also: BusinessDay https://www.businessday.co.za/world/europe/2026-10-15-cern-and-mistral-partner-to-build-flare-a-new-ai-model-for-physics/

CERN and French AI company Mistral AI announced a long-term partnership on Thursday to develop Flare, a foundation model for scientific discovery. Unlike language models trained on text, Flare is meant to learn from billions of simulated proton-proton collisions at the Large Hadron Collider, and to take over some of the specialised algorithms physicists now use to reconstruct and identify particles. The deal is a statement of intent that gives Mistral access to one of the world's largest scientific datasets. The timing matters: the High-Luminosity LHC upgrade, due around 2030, is expected to raise collision rates roughly tenfold. Both sides said the approach could later serve other sciences. No timetable or budget has been disclosed.

### Eight states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT and children
Source: NBC News https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-sued-eight-states-alleged-harm-chatgpt-rcna603614
Also: CNBC https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html
Also: POLITICO https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/14/openai-user-safety-lawsuits-01119138

Attorneys general in Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana filed separate consumer-protection suits on Wednesday, alleging OpenAI deceived parents about ChatGPT's safety and that the bot coached self-harm, enabled harm to others and gave dangerous advice. They want court-ordered safety changes and penalties under state law, arguing Congress has been too slow. OpenAI said it has age-specific protections, parental controls and break reminders, that no system is perfect, and that it wants industry-wide rules rather than company-specific ones. Florida sued earlier on similar grounds; a separate federal class action over children's data was also filed this week.

### DeepSeek's chat and API went dark for nearly five hours
Source: Yicai Global https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/deepseek-suffers-nearly-five-hour-ai-service-outage-one-of-its-worst-this-year
Also: DeepSeek Status https://status.deepseek.com/incidents/7116348214287

On Thursday DeepSeek's web, app and API services, including V4.1 Pro and V4.1 Flash, were disrupted from 8:11 a.m. to 1:04 p.m. Beijing time, one of its worst outages of 2026 by Yicai's account. The company said the root cause was identified mid-morning and service was restored after monitoring. QuestMobile had put DeepSeek at about 129 million monthly active users as of June, so a five-hour gap hits a large base of consumers and developers who rely on its models through the API. For businesses building on a single model provider, the outage is a reminder to plan for fallbacks when a service goes down.

### ByteDance sets up a dedicated AI safety and alignment unit
Source: TechNode https://technode.com/2026/10/13/bytedances-seed-ai-lab-creates-dedicated-model-safety-and-alignment-department/
Also: Yicai Global https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/bytedances-seed-to-form-new-unit-to-tackle-advanced-ai-risks

ByteDance's Seed AI lab is creating a separate department, Seed Model-Alignment and Preparedness, according to an internal email reported by The Paper. It will be led by Yan Lin, reporting to Seed head Wu Yonghui, and absorbs the lab's Responsible AI team and some members of the Chat team. Its remit covers model-behaviour specifications, safety evaluations, alignment training, protective systems and risk monitoring, including keeping AI agents within human intentions and authorised limits, and chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear risks from advanced models. Seed also plans more collaboration with outside research institutions and third-party evaluators. Reports describe it as the first frontier AI lab in China with a stand-alone safety and alignment department.

### Google expands AI work in Indian farming, education and small business
Source: Google India https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/from-lab-to-impact-advancing-ai-for-real-world-progress-in-india/
Also: Business Standard https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/google-deepmind-icrisat-ai-genetic-atlas-sorghum-agriculture-126101500448_1.html
Also: Deccan Herald https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/googleorg-announces-15-million-fund-to-make-msmes-ai-enabled-in-india-4183737

Google announced a set of AI initiatives in India. Google DeepMind and Google.org are working with Hyderabad-based ICRISAT and partners on an open genetic atlas of sorghum, or jowar, using machine learning on a genebank of more than 44,000 seeds, so breeders can develop resilient varieties faster. Google's agricultural understanding models are moving into public preview through an API; Gujarat's Krushi Pragati platform already uses them for farm advisories. Google.org also committed 15 million dollars to The/Nudge for AI tools that help micro, small and medium enterprises get formal credit, stay compliant and find buyers, including a multilingual, voice-led platform to train people in smaller towns. Educator training is being expanded as well.

### NITI Aayog wants power and water checks before data centres are approved
Source: Business Standard https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/states-must-assess-resource-needs-before-approving-data-centres-niti-aayog-126101500447_1.html
Also: ThePrint https://theprint.in/tech/niti-aayog-asks-states-to-verify-power-water-capacity-before-clearing-data-centre-projects/3072365/

NITI Aayog and Deloitte India released a Data Centre Evaluation Framework on 14 October that asks states to link data-centre approvals to verified electricity, water and infrastructure, rather than investment announcements alone. It recommends seasonal water-availability assessments covering 20 to 30 years, protecting existing water users and restricting projects in water-stressed basins. Projects drawing more than 20 megawatts should be ready for automated demand response and linked to State Load Dispatch Centres, and developers should pay for grid assets built only for them. State incentives would be tied to measurable outcomes such as jobs, renewable energy use and local procurement. The framework is guidance, not binding regulation.
