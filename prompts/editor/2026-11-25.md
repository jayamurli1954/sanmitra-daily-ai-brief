# WORLD

### UN Security Council told AI is shrinking the time to head off escalation
Source: UN Meetings Coverage https://press.un.org/en/2026/sc16518.doc.htm
Also: UN News https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168866

At a Security Council open debate on emerging technology and conflict, Microsoft corporate vice president Frederick Humphries cited the company's 2026 Digital Defense Report: the median time from a newly found software weakness to a working attack is now under 24 hours. He said AI speeds up both attackers and defenders, and leaves less time to verify events and prevent escalation. Many speakers called for human control and legal oversight of military AI. Speaking for India, Ambassador Harish Parvathaneni said responsibility for the use of lethal autonomous weapons must always rest with humans. He also criticised the use of export controls and licensing for "strategic one-upmanship" that treats the Global South as mere consumers of the technology.

### Tidal will delete wholly AI-generated music from 9 December
Source: Digital Music News https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/11/24/tidal-bans-ai-generated-music/
Also: TIDAL Support https://support.tidal.com/hc/en-us/articles/48031883413521-AI-Policy

From 9 December, the streaming service will reject and remove tracks made entirely by AI, about 27 million songs, and will stop distributors from delivering them. Tidal told Digital Music News that 43% of tracks delivered in the past 90 days were detected as 100% AI-generated, a company figure. Tracks where AI is only one of the tools used are still allowed. The move goes further than Tidal's policy from June, which tagged wholly AI-generated music and stopped it earning royalties but kept it on the service.

# USA

### OpenAI says GPT-6 models can design proteins without specialist tools
Source: OpenAI https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities
Also: Nature https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03836-z

OpenAI says its GPT-6 family can design new protein shapes and sequences using only reasoning and code. In lab tests with Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2 designs showed signs of binding across five targets. OpenAI notes that specialised models still helped supply sequences and pick candidates. These are company and preprint results, not peer-reviewed findings, and any drug made this way is still a long way off.

### OpenAI posts a claimed proof that the matrix multiplication exponent is 9/4
Source: OpenAI https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026

A new OpenAI preprint, dated 24 November, argues that the exponent of square matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4 over every field. That would rule out the long-held hope that the exponent could reach 2. OpenAI had already published an upper bound of 9/4 in October; the new paper claims it is also the floor. Only part of the argument is checked in the Lean proof assistant: the formal proof covers division-free circuits computing polynomial identities. The paper has not been peer-reviewed, so treat it as a claim until specialists check it.

### Cognition releases SWE-2.1, a coding agent model built on Kimi K3
Source: Cognition https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2-1

Cognition says SWE-2.1 was trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot's open Kimi K3 model. In its own benchmarks, the model roughly doubles SWE-2's scores, for example 50.0 against 24.2 on Terminal-Bench 4.1, but it still trails the top frontier models on several tests. These are the company's own figures and have not been checked independently.

# CHINA

### China says its AI core industry passed 750 billion yuan in the first half
Source: Bastille Post https://www.bastillepost.com/global/article/6396250-chinas-ai-core-industry-expected-to-exceed-750-billion-yuan-in-h1
Also: Xinhua https://english.news.cn/20261125/e1414216c1e44e03b72e442014b0951b/c.html

At a Beijing press conference on AI standards, Yao Jia of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology said China's core AI industry was expected to exceed 750 billion yuan, about 112.5 billion US dollars, in the first half of 2026, with more than 6,600 core companies. The market regulator SAMR added that China has led 78 international AI standards, 29 of them foundational, covering areas such as data governance and security. These are official figures.

# ASIA

### Japan sets out to be the leading AI nation after the US and China
Source: The Japan Times https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/11/25/japan/ai-nation-aim/

Japan will add this goal to its three revised national security documents later this year, the Jiji news agency reports. The outline calls for cutting Japan's reliance on other countries for AI, and for heavy investment in automated driving and physical AI, meaning AI that controls robots and machines. The ruling Liberal Democratic Party broadly approved the outline on Wednesday.

### Korea expects its next national AI model to match China's open-weight models
Source: MoneyToday https://www.mt.co.kr/en/tech/2026/11/25/2026112510555089302

Science minister Bae Kyung-hoon said the third version of Korea's homegrown AI foundation model, due in February, should reach the level of China's current open-weight models. He also warned that Korea's economy leans too heavily on chips.

# INDIA

### MeitY's AI consultation paper is in its final stages
Source: Storyboard18 https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/meity-prepares-consultation-paper-on-ai-regulation-for-india-ws-l-115473.htm

The secretary of India's Ministry of Electronics and IT said the ministry's consultation paper on AI is nearly done and should be out soon. It will be the starting point for talks with stakeholders on how India regulates AI, and the government does not plan to rush into rules. In October, IT minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said the paper would focus on deepfakes and safety.
