# WORLD

### UN Security Council told AI is shrinking the time to head off escalation
Source: UN Meetings Coverage https://press.un.org/en/2026/sc16518.doc.htm
Also: UN News https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168866

At a Security Council open debate on emerging technology and conflict, Microsoft corporate VP Frederick Humphries cited the company's 2026 Digital Defense Report: the median time from a newly found weakness to a working attack is now under 24 hours. He said AI speeds up both attackers and defenders and leaves less time to verify events and prevent escalation. Many speakers called for human control and legal oversight of military AI.

### 81 MEPs want the EU's "nudifier" ban enforced from day one
Source: MLex https://www.mlex.com/mlex/artificial-intelligence/articles/2559638/enforce-eu-nudifier-ban-promptly-and-explore-meta-s-ad-role-lawmakers-urge

A group of 81 European Parliament members want AI providers and platforms that still offer or promote tools that digitally undress people without consent to be investigated as soon as the AI Act ban applies on 2 December. They also want Meta's role in advertising these tools examined.

### Tidal will delete wholly AI-generated music from 9 December
Source: Digital Music News https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/11/24/tidal-bans-ai-generated-music/
Also: TIDAL Support https://support.tidal.com/hc/en-us/articles/48031883413521-AI-Policy

From 9 December, the streaming service will reject and remove tracks made entirely by AI, about 27 million songs, and will stop distributors from delivering them. Tidal told Digital Music News that 43% of tracks delivered in the past 90 days were detected as 100% AI-generated (a company figure). Tracks where AI is only one of the tools used are still allowed.

# USA

### OpenAI says GPT-6 models can design proteins without specialist tools
Source: OpenAI https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities
Also: Nature https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03836-z

OpenAI says its GPT-6 family can design new protein shapes and sequences using only reasoning and code. In lab tests with Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2 designs showed signs of binding across five targets. OpenAI notes that specialised models still helped supply sequences and pick candidates. These are company and preprint results, and a drug is still a long way off.

### OpenAI posts a claimed proof that the matrix multiplication exponent is 9/4
Source: OpenAI (GitHub) https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026
Also: TopFive https://topfive.ai/2026/11/25/

A new OpenAI preprint argues that the exponent of square matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4 over every field. That would rule out the long-held hope that it could reach 2. Only part of it is checked in Lean, so treat it as a claim until specialists review it.

### Anthropic lets MCP servers ship skills and run long jobs in Claude
Source: Claude by Anthropic https://claude.com/resources/articles/skills-over-mcp-tasks-and-more
Also: The New Stack https://thenewstack.io/mcp-skills-tasks-claude/

Skills over MCP is now in public beta, so a connector can bring its own playbook along with its tools. Tasks (long background jobs) and Elicitation (Claude stops to ask the user for input) are now generally available, and file uploads are coming soon.

### Cognition releases SWE-2.1, a coding agent model built on Kimi K3
Source: Cognition https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2-1

Cognition says SWE-2.1 was trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot's Kimi K3. In its own benchmarks, the model roughly doubles SWE-2's scores, for example 50.0 vs 24.2 on Terminal-Bench 4.1, but still trails the top frontier models on several tests.

# CHINA

### China says its AI core industry passed 750 billion yuan in the first half
Source: Bastille Post https://www.bastillepost.com/global/article/6396250-chinas-ai-core-industry-expected-to-exceed-750-billion-yuan-in-h1
Also: China.com.cn http://news.china.com.cn/txt/2026-11/25/content_118788804.shtml
Also: Xinhua https://english.news.cn/20261125/e1414216c1e44e03b72e442014b0951b/c.html

At a Beijing press conference on AI standards, Yao Jia of the industry ministry (MIIT) said China's core AI industry was expected to exceed 750 billion yuan (about US$112.5 billion) in H1 2026, with more than 6,600 core companies. The market regulator SAMR added that China has led 78 international AI standards, 29 of them foundational, covering areas such as data governance and security. These are official figures.

# ASIA

### Japan sets out to be the leading AI nation after the US and China
Source: The Japan Times https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/11/25/japan/ai-nation-aim/

Japan will add this goal to its three revised national security documents later this year, Jiji reports. The outline calls for cutting Japan's AI reliance on other countries and investing heavily in automated driving and physical AI. The ruling LDP broadly approved it on Wednesday.

### Korea expects its next national AI model to match China's open-weight models
Source: MoneyToday https://www.mt.co.kr/en/tech/2026/11/25/2026112510555089302

Science minister Bae Kyung-hoon said the third version of Korea's homegrown foundation model, due in February, should reach the level of China's current open-weight models. He also warned that the economy leans too heavily on chips.

# INDIA

### At the UN, India says humans must stay responsible for lethal autonomous weapons
Source: The Hindu https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/india-warns-against-using-tech-for-strategic-one-upmanship-calls-for-inclusive-ai-governance/article71741155.ece

Speaking at the same Security Council debate, Ambassador Harish Parvathaneni said responsibility for using lethal autonomous weapons must always rest with humans. He criticised the use of export controls and licensing for "strategic one-upmanship" that treats the Global South as mere consumers.
