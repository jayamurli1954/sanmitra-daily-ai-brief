# USA

### DeepMind's AlphaProof Code formally verifies parts of Signal's encryption
Google DeepMind has introduced AlphaProof Code, an agentic AI system that produced machine-checked Lean proofs of key security properties in the real production code behind the Signal messaging protocol, including forward secrecy and authentication, all the way down to the compiled machine code. The project combined AI agents with mathematical proof techniques and human-written specifications, and generated millions of lines of proof code. It is a research proof of concept: the proofs cover selected properties, not every possible attack. Still, it is a strong sign that AI can make formal software verification practical, moving beyond finding bugs to giving mathematical evidence that specific security guarantees hold, with implications for cryptography and critical infrastructure.
Source: Google DeepMind https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/

### Google makes Gemini 4 Carbon widely available
Gemini 4 Carbon is now available through the paid Gemini API, Google AI Studio and Gemini for business, and is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search. Google pitches it at long-running agent tasks such as complex coding, research and security work, and calls it its strongest model, which is the company's own description. Launch pricing is two dollars per million input tokens and ten dollars per million output tokens. The wider release puts Google's top model in front of developers and businesses at a time when rivals in the US and China are shipping new frontier models almost weekly.
Source: Google https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/

### OpenAI opens ChatGPT to business profiles and business agents
OpenAI has introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, a beta that lets companies claim and manage their profiles inside ChatGPT. Through a Business Hub, eligible firms can update company details, supply product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated insights such as mentions and website clicks. OpenAI is also introducing Business Agents, which let customers ask a company's agent about its products and services. The first rollout covers eligible US e-commerce and online-service businesses, and OpenAI says individual customer conversations are not shared with businesses. Companies may soon need to manage how they appear inside AI assistants, much as they manage their presence on search engines and marketplaces today.
Source: OpenAI https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/

### Adobe brings AI agents into creative workflows
Adobe announced a broad expansion of AI tools across Creative Cloud, Firefly and GenStudio. Its Creative Agent is being extended to run multi-step creative workflows, and new integrations will let third-party agents work inside Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro and After Effects. An expanded partnership with Google brings Adobe tools into more Google products and makes Google's AI models more available inside Adobe's apps. The direction is shifting from AI that generates single images or clips to agents that coordinate several stages of professional production, which could cut turnaround time for media and marketing teams while keeping familiar creative software at the centre.
Source: Adobe https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-powers-new-era-of-creativity

### Fireworks AI details a security incident that reached secrets and model weights
AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI has published further findings from an October security incident that began at a third-party cloud provider. The company says attackers reached internal development machines and accessed some account and billing information, and for a limited number of accounts also secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights. Fireworks says it has contained the incident, hardened credentials and is still investigating, and that it has seen no impact on its inference and training services, which keep no customer data by default. The case shows that AI security has to cover the whole supply chain of cloud hosts, datasets and development tools, not just the model.
Source: Fireworks AI https://fireworks.ai/security/bulletins/october-2026-security-incident-update

# WORLD

### Hackers used AI agents to steal Tajik government documents, researchers say
A Kazakhstan-linked hacking group used an autonomous AI-agent framework to steal more than 2,000 national-security-related documents from Tajikistan, according to researchers at the cybersecurity firm CloudSEK. The group reportedly used agents built on the open-source Hermes framework for reconnaissance, intrusion support, writing custom software and sorting stolen files. Investigators found the operation after the attackers accidentally left a server exposed with their operational data on it. The findings suggest AI in cyberattacks is moving beyond phishing emails and malicious code toward agents that coordinate several stages of an attack, raising the bar for defenders.
Source: Reuters https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/kazakhstan-linked-hackers-used-ai-steal-tajik-government-documents-researchers-2026-11-11/

### Dassault Systemes to set up an ASEAN industrial AI lab in Singapore
French software group Dassault Systemes signed a memorandum of understanding with Singapore's Economic Development Board to set up its ASEAN Industrial AI Virtual Twin Lab in the city-state. The lab will apply AI and virtual twins, detailed digital models of products, factories and processes, to manufacturing and industry across Southeast Asia. Dassault plans to hire more than 30 AI professionals there over five years. The move adds to Singapore's push to become the region's hub for applied industrial AI.
Source: TNGlobal https://technode.global/2026/11/11/frances-dassault-systemes-to-set-up-asean-industrial-ai-virtual-twin-lab-in-singapore/

### South Korea jumps to 10th in IMD's digital and AI competitiveness ranking
South Korea's science ministry says the country climbed eight places to 10th of 70 economies in the latest IMD World Digital Competitiveness ranking, which measures how ready economies are to adopt digital technology and AI. The United States ranked first, Singapore was second in the Asia-Pacific region, China seventh and Japan 19th. The jump follows heavy government spending on AI computing, talent and public-sector adoption, as Seoul pushes to become one of the world's top three AI powers.

### Andhra Pradesh clears a Rs 31,387 crore AI data centre near Visakhapatnam
The Andhra Pradesh Cabinet approved a 512 megawatt green AI data centre by AM Intelligence at Bheemunipatnam, near Visakhapatnam, worth 31,387 crore rupees. It also cleared land at Bhogapuram for Reliance's giga-scale AI data centre and a 570 crore rupee Epack plant that will make equipment for AI data centres. The approvals strengthen the Visakhapatnam coast's position as one of India's main clusters for AI computing infrastructure.
