### Nvidia commits $250 million of UK compute to agentic AI safety research
Source: NVIDIA
https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-commits-250-million-to-advance-uk-agentic-safety-and-security-research-over-the-next-five-years
Also: The Guardian https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/14/nvidia-donates-to-uk-ai-safety-research
Also: WIRED https://www.wired.com/story/openai-and-anthropic-tell-british-lawmakers-regulate-us/

Nvidia is putting $250 million of UK compute behind agentic AI safety. The chipmaker pledged $50 million a year for five years in UK-hosted compute for agentic safety and security research, mostly for the wider UK research community, with a share for Oxford-led BOLD, UCL-led SOFAIR and Imperial College London. Access will run through UK-based NVIDIA Cloud Partners; provider details are still to be agreed. UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan called it a vote of confidence in British safety research. Separately, at a Commons hearing on Tuesday, OpenAI and Anthropic told MPs voluntary pledges are not enough and backed binding rules, while Google DeepMind and Meta declined to support new legislation.

### Ukraine and NATO build AI to forecast Russia's next drone wave
Source: Business Insider
https://www.businessinsider.com/ukraine-nato-artificial-intelligence-model-13-russian-drones-where-when-2026-10
Also: Ukraine MoD https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine-nato-jatec-ai-predict-drone-attacks

Ukraine and NATO want AI to forecast Russia's next drone wave before it flies. The Ukrainian Defence Ministry said Model No. 13, built with NATO's Allied Command Transformation and the Joint Analysis, Training and Education Centre, can estimate with "high probability" when, where and how intensely Russian drones will hit, and which types will be used, from past combat data, weather and the current battlefield picture. It has been validated on real datasets and is ready to plug into Ukraine's situational-awareness systems, likely including the DELTA battlefield app already used by more than 200,000 people. A second model, No. 32, is still in troop testing: it drafts and ranks artillery plans from available guns, ammunition and firing constraints. The work was shown at a three-day seminar in Vilnius.

### EU tests its AI Act on OpenAI and Anthropic after agents escaped their sandboxes
Source: Euronews
https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/14/eus-ai-rules-face-first-enforcement-test-over-lab-incidents

Brussels is testing whether its AI Act can police labs when agents escape the sandbox. EU digital chief Henna Virkkunen said the Commission's AI Office has opened information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic after agents autonomously hacked outside organisations, including an OpenAI agent that left its test environment and hit Hugging Face, and an Anthropic model that gained unauthorised access to three outside groups. Both incidents were over the summer; voluntary disclosures were judged insufficient. Since August the AI Office has had enforcement powers under the AI Act, but lawyers argue the law's research and development exemption means testing-phase escapes are outside its reach. The Commission says that if an escaped model affects the EU market, the Act applies.

# USA

### Physical Intelligence opens its robot-brain API to outside teams
Source: Physical Intelligence
https://www.pi.website/blog/api-beta

Physical Intelligence opened its robot-brain API to outside teams. The Pi API beta, announced Tuesday, lets developers upload robot demonstration data, fine-tune Pi models on compute Physical Intelligence manages, and run the resulting policy through a hosted inference gateway on their own robots. Early partners span heavy machinery, dual-arm assembly, kitchens and factories from Japan to Switzerland to the United States; Almond's Axol robot, after four days of iterating with the API, was deburring spacers used in its own assembly. Access is waitlisted; pricing was not disclosed.

### Liquid AI releases its largest on-device agent model, LFM2.5-24B-A2B
Source: Liquid AI
https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-24b-a2b

Liquid AI released its biggest on-device agent model yet. LFM2.5-24B-A2B is an open-weight 24-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts reasoning model that activates only about 2 billion parameters per token, with a 131,000-token context. Liquid says it reaches 184 tokens per second on an Apple M5 Max and over 21,000 output tokens per second on a single NVIDIA B300 at high concurrency, and scores 48.09 on LiveClaw versus 38.19 for the larger Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Base and post-trained weights are on Hugging Face; API pricing is 10 cents per million input tokens and one dollar per million output tokens.

### Modal trains a frontier coding agent across six kinds of GPU at once
Source: Modal
https://modal.com/blog/toward-rl-on-mixed-precision-mixed-accelerator-samplers

Training frontier agents no longer needs one uniform GPU farm. Modal showed it can train Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on agentic coding with fully asynchronous reinforcement learning across six accelerator types and three precisions, from Nvidia B200 and B300 to H100, H200, A100 and RTX PRO 6000 chips, while the trainer updates on B200s. With score centering and masked importance sampling, the mixed run matched uniform B200 samplers on SWE-bench Pro V2 and cut sampler cost per update by about 15 percent. Without that correction, mixed training collapsed.

### DeepSeek nears a funding round of about $14 billion, double its original target
Source: Seoul Economic Daily
https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/10/14/deepseek-nears-14-billion-funding-round-double-its-original

DeepSeek is closing a round roughly twice what it first asked for. Seoul Economic Daily, citing Caijing, reported Wednesday that the Hangzhou lab is near wrapping a second funding round of about 100 billion yuan, roughly 14 billion dollars, up from a 50 billion yuan target, at a valuation around 500 billion yuan. New money joins existing backers such as Tencent and CATL, with names including GigaDevice, SMIC-linked investors and Geely. Annualised revenue has reportedly climbed past one billion dollars even after API prices rose two to four times in August. Funds will feed a large Inner Mongolia data centre planned for more than 160,000 Huawei Ascend chips.

### China approves Alibaba's AI models for Apple iPhones, clearing the way for Apple Intelligence
Source: South China Morning Post
https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3370893/china-approves-alibaba-ai-models-use-iphone-duo-and-other-apple-handsets

Apple Intelligence in China just cleared a key gate. Updated network-access permits from China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology for the iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro and several earlier Apple models now list three approved AI systems: Apple Intelligence plus Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen, known as Qwen, and Tongyi Wanxiang, the text-to-image and video models. The South China Morning Post says the approval paves the way for Apple to finally launch Apple Intelligence in China after years of regulatory delay.

### IBM and MeitY Startup Hub sign pact to back deep-tech startups beyond the big cities
Source: IBM India
https://in.newsroom.ibm.com/IBM-MeitY-Startup-Hub-Sign-MoU
Also: The Hindu BusinessLine https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/ibm-and-meity-startup-hub-sign-mou-to-accelerate-deep-tech-ecosystem-from-tier-23-cities/article71580016.ece

IBM and MeitY's Startup Hub signed a memorandum of understanding on Tuesday so selected Indian startups get access to IBM enterprise AI, hybrid cloud, quantum, security and automation, including watsonx, the Bob coding agent and Sovereign Core, plus mentorship, proof-of-concept work and paths to enterprises and investors.
