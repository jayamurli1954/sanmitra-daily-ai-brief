# USA

### Ex-Palantir execs launch Dream with $17M from a16z to build AI world models for construction
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/ex-palantir-execs-launch-dream-with-17m-from-a16z-to-build-ai-world-models-for-construction/

Dream, a startup founded by former Palantir executives Andy Elder and Arjun Mathur, has come out of stealth with 17 million dollars in seed funding led by Andreessen Horowitz, with SV Angel, XYZ and Zigg Capital joining. It is building world models that automate building design and engineering, aiming to replace older architecture software. The model is trained on more than 100 million square feet of building data from US permit archives and follows real physics and construction standards, down to plumbing and air-conditioning systems. Architects, engineers and developers already use it for concept designs and cost estimates on 10 active projects in Texas and California.

### Wabi, the AI agent that builds mini apps, now valued at $1.25B after latest round
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/wabi-the-ai-agent-that-builds-mini-apps-now-valued-at-1-25b-after-latest-round/

Wabi, the AI app from Replika founder Eugenia Kuyda, has raised 55 million dollars at a 1.25 billion dollar valuation. IVP led the round, with Andreessen Horowitz, Forerunner and the Amazon Alexa Fund joining, along with Shopify chief Tobi Lütke and Salesforce chief Marc Benioff as angel investors. Wabi started as a tool for building apps from a prompt, then in September turned into an AI messenger it now calls a personal agent. The agent builds small custom apps on request, for meal planning, fitness plans, tracking local restaurants or trivia games. The round shows how strongly investors are betting on personal AI agents.

### Deepgram acquires Pipecat-maker Daily to bolster its voice AI infrastructure
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/deepgram-acquires-pipecat-maker-daily-to-bolster-its-voice-ai-infrastructure/

Voice AI company Deepgram has agreed to buy Daily, the company behind Pipecat, an open-source framework for real-time voice and video AI. The price was not disclosed, and Deepgram says Pipecat will stay open source. Pipecat stitches together live audio and video streams with speech recognition, a language model and speech synthesis, with the aim of cutting the delay before an AI agent answers. Daily also runs hosted infrastructure for voice and video calls. Alongside the deal, Deepgram is launching the Real-Time Context Standard, an open protocol built with the Pipecat team. It is a sign that voice agents are becoming a core battleground for AI infrastructure.

### Nvidia says Megaspeed dealings were normal after probe report
Source: Tech in Asia
https://www.techinasia.com/news/nvidia-megaspeed-dealings-normal-probe-report

Nvidia has pushed back after a probe report raised questions about its dealings with Megaspeed. The case shows how closely governments and the chip industry now watch where advanced AI chips end up, as export controls tighten and smuggling investigations spread across Asia.

### AI inference chip startup Nano-core Chip raises over RMB 1 billion in Series C2 funding
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/10/30/ai-inference-chip-startup-nano-core-chip-raises-over-rmb-1-billion-in-series-c2-funding/

Hangzhou-based Nano-core Chip, which designs chips for running AI models, has raised more than 1 billion yuan in a Series C2 round led by JianTou Investment, with Hangzhou Capital and Kaitai Capital joining. It follows a 1 billion yuan Series C1 round in September. Founded in 2021 out of a Peking University research institute in Zhejiang, the company combines near-memory and in-memory computing with RISC-V processors, for both devices and cloud servers. It says its first three chips have been taped out, with customer samples and first volume shipments planned for early 2027. China is pouring money into home-grown inference chips as access to Nvidia's top parts stays restricted.

### Freshworks Acquires AI Startup Robusta To Strengthen IT Operations Platform
Source: Inc42
https://inc42.com/buzz/freshworks-acquires-ai-startup-robusta-to-strengthen-it-operations-platform/

Freshworks has acquired Israel-based AI startup Robusta Dev for an undisclosed amount. Robusta, founded by Natan Yellin and Arik Alon, uses AI agents to work out what caused an outage, by analysing monitoring data, infrastructure details and recent code or configuration changes. It also maps how a failure spreads to connected services and recommends fixes, which engineers review and approve before anything reaches live systems. Freshworks, which has large engineering teams in India, will build this into Freshservice, its IT service management platform, and link it with FireHydrant, the incident-management startup it bought last year. The goal is fewer manual investigations and faster recovery when systems break.
