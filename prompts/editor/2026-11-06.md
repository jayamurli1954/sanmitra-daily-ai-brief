# INDIA

### India's CERT-In asks organisations to audit what their AI agents can access and do
India's national cybersecurity agency, CERT-In, has called on organisations to test whether their AI agents can expose confidential information or carry out actions they were never authorised to take. Its AI security auditing guidelines ask for tight permission controls, a record of every action an agent takes, a way to interrupt an agent in an emergency, and human approval before any consequential step. The guidelines also cover prompt injection, where malicious instructions hidden in documents, emails or web pages try to take control of an agent.

Why it matters: Indian businesses are starting to give AI agents access to email, documents, internal systems and financial records. CERT-In's guidance makes agent permissions and audit trails a basic security control, not an afterthought.
