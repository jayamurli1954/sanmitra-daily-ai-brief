# WORLD

### UAE launches FedAI to run half of government work with AI agents within two years
Lead: yes
Source: The National
https://is.gd/42IOn7

The United Arab Emirates has unveiled FedAI, a national ecosystem for agentic AI in government, with 17 local and international partners signing on, The National reports. The government says it wants AI agents handling 50 percent of its sectors, services and processes within two years. An AI concierge called Mira is due from March, letting residents use government services in their own language. These are targets the government has set, not results it has achieved so far.

### Canada adds C$700 million to help small firms pay for AI compute
Source: Government of Canada
https://is.gd/JeYHZx

The Canadian government has expanded its AI Compute Access Fund with a top-up of C$700 million, according to an announcement from Ottawa. It has opened a new call for proposals and named 45 more companies that will receive support. The government has also raised the incentive for firms that buy computing power from sovereign Canadian providers, as part of a push to keep more AI infrastructure in the country.

### Nebius starts invisible watermarks on AI-generated text for the EU AI Act
Source: 8digits
https://is.gd/N8xEwu

Nebius Token Factory is adding invisible statistical watermarks to text produced through its endpoints, rolling the change out from 27 November to 1 December, 8digits reports. Nebius says it is meeting the duty under Article 50 of the EU AI Act to mark content generated by AI. It is one of the first large inference providers to watermark text in this way, and some developers have objected to the change.

### AI agent's cardless pizza order is a first for the UK
Source: The Guardian
https://is.gd/obNv8K

An order of nine mini margherita pizzas from Papa John's has become the first purchase in Britain made by an AI agent using cardless payment, the Guardian reports. The order was placed during sandbox trials run by the Financial Conduct Authority to test the technology, with Lloyds Bank and the London payments firm TrueLayer taking part. TrueLayer moves money directly between bank accounts without cards, and said it was the first time an AI agent had paid a UK retailer for a real customer's order. Chief executive Francesco Simoneschi said the payment needed no account numbers and no password.

### Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34051

In a paper titled "Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability," the researchers look at linear attention. This is a technique that lets AI models handle long sequences efficiently by keeping a fixed-size memory, known as a state. One design, Gated DeltaNet-2, gives finer control over erasing and writing that memory, but it fails during long-context training. The researchers traced the failure to a mismatch in how erasure reads and changes the memory. That mismatch lets the state grow exponentially, even when nothing new is written. Their fix uses symmetric erasure to stop the state expanding, along with decay-regulated residual writing to keep writing flexible.

# USA

### OpenAI fixes a usage bug, so GPT-6.2 limits now arrive sooner
Source: BleepingComputer
https://is.gd/S7lsrh

OpenAI says a bug at launch had been undercounting how much subscribers used GPT-6.2, BleepingComputer reports. With the bug fixed, users will reach their limits about 40 percent faster on Astra and about 25 percent faster on Sol than they did last week. OpenAI is not charging for the usage that went uncounted, and it is offering subscribers an extra reset of their usage allowance.

### OpenAI's GPT-Live-1.1 takes first place on a live phone-call benchmark
Source: Voice Arena
https://voicearena.com/jarvis-bench

OpenAI's GPT-Live-1.1, a full-duplex voice model that can listen and speak at the same time, has debuted in first place on Jarvis Bench, a Voice Arena leaderboard that scores real phone calls. There is a catch. Human raters still preferred human agents, and they scored Google's Gemini higher on how well calls were handled.

### Anthropic rebuilds its Messages API in Rust, cutting CPU use by 70 percent
Source: Analytics India Magazine
https://is.gd/EkyZC0

Anthropic has rebuilt its Messages API in the Rust programming language, moving away from Python, Analytics India Magazine reports. The change cut processor use per request by about 70 percent and memory use by more than 80 percent. Traffic to the API grew 1,800 times over two years, and the Python service had become expensive to run. The project also exposed a weakness in code written by AI: the new version passed 98 percent of the existing integration tests, but when first checked against live production traffic it matched only about a quarter of the inputs.

# CHINA

### Nvidia Blackwell racks reported at a Shanghai site tied to blacklisted Megaspeed
Source: Quartz
https://is.gd/82pF4X

Two Chinese companies installed an Nvidia GB300 NVL72 rack and GB200 servers at a Shanghai data centre linked to Megaspeed, Quartz reports, citing Bloomberg. ### Ant Group open-sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design for design work
Source: Hugging Face
https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ming-Image-0.2-Design

Ant Group's inclusionAI team has released the weights of Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6-billion-parameter image model built for design work, on Hugging Face. # ASIA

### South Korea declares an emergency response to AI-powered cyberattacks
Source: Korea JoongAng Daily
https://is.gd/WNT8oB

South Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission has switched on an emergency response system as hacking powered by AI surges, Korea JoongAng Daily reports. Reported breaches of personal data in the first 10 months of the year are more than double last year's total. Public agencies face three months of inspections, and breaches that could have been prevented will draw strict penalties.

### Japan's JERA in talks to put US hyperscaler data centres beside Tokyo power plants
Source: DCD
https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/japans-jera-in-talks-with-us-hyperscalers-to-colocate-data-centers-at-thermal-power-plants-in-japan/

# INDIA

### Bhargavastra counter-drone-swarm system clears trials at Pokhran
Source: NDTV
https://is.gd/TrRvdA

Solar Defence and Aerospace says Bhargavastra, a vehicle-mounted system for defending against swarms of drones, finished in-house development trials at Pokhran between 20 and 24 November, NDTV reports. The system links AI-enabled command and control with radars, radio-frequency detectors and electro-optical tracking, and fires rockets and guided micro-missiles. These were the company's own trials, not acceptance tests by the army.

https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/india-has-adopted-ai-faster-than-it-can-measure-own-or-govern-it-et-ai-adoption-index/articleshow/136221444.cms

Interim results from the ET AI Adoption Index, run with Zinnov and FICCI, show that Indian companies are adopting AI faster than they can measure or govern it, The Economic Times reports. ### Airtel to invest Rs 1,200 crore in three data centres across Assam
Source: Analytics India Magazine
https://is.gd/i9Dqcg

Bharti Airtel plans to invest 1,200 crore rupees in three data centres across Assam, with a combined capacity of 40 megawatts, Analytics India Magazine reports. Of that, 700 crore rupees will go to its Guwahati facility and 500 crore rupees to two planned centres in Jorhat and Silchar. The Assam government and Airtel have signed a memorandum of understanding for the Jorhat and Silchar sites, which are expected to create about 164 direct jobs.
