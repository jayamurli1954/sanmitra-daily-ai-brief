# WORLD

### The Pentagon lays out how it will stand up an Autonomous Warfare Command
Lead: yes
In an open letter to the Joint Force and industry on 21 October, Project Agincourt leaders Owen West and Senior Chief Max Strasiser set out the operational roadmap for the Autonomous Warfare Command (AUTOWARCOM), a planned four-star command for unmanned and autonomous warfare that the Department of War is targeting for 1 October 2027. The letter says procurement decisions and money will be pushed directly to frontline units, and success will be judged by how fast a battlefield problem gets a fielded, scalable fix rather than by paperwork milestones; it also warns that "urgency does not excuse imprecision". It is a roadmap, not a stood-up command. (Themes: AI policy and regulation; technology trends)

### Claude designed protein binders against scorpion neurotoxins
Anthropic said an internal research model working in Claude Science ran a mostly autonomous 64-hour design campaign, generating 318,484 candidate proteins against five scorpion neurotoxins and picking 180 for lab tests; with 31 added by the team, contract labs Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience tested 211, and 17 met Anthropic's criteria as binders, each against a single toxin. The two strongest bound the Mexican scorpion toxin Cn2 at single-digit nanomolar strength and appear to hit the same site as a mouse-protective antibody. Anthropic is clear this is not an antivenom: nothing has been tested against real venom, in cells or in animals, and no multi-toxin design worked. All 211 designs and data are being released. (Theme: AI breakthroughs)

Source: Anthropic https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design

# CHINA

### ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent reportedly order $35B+ of Nvidia cards
Reuters reports that the three companies have ordered more than 3 million of Nvidia's new RTX PRO 5500 cards at about $11,400 each, mainly for AI inference, because it is among the most powerful Nvidia chips still legal to sell into China. Total Chinese orders reportedly exceed 6 million while Nvidia may make only about 3 million globally in 2027, and one source said partners might get just 5–10% of what they asked for. Samples are expected in December and deliveries from early 2027. The figures come from unnamed sources; the companies did not comment. (Themes: technology trends; AI industry and startup news)

### SenseTime open-sources a robot test harness and a 27B "robot brain"
SenseTime released SenseNova-RoboRSI, a framework that packages 12 simulated robot benchmarks behind one API, alongside a 27B embodied model preview. In its own tests GPT-6 Astra could saturate some benchmarks given long runs, but stacking bowls took about 131 minutes and roughly $19 of inference; SenseTime says its 27B model does embodied tasks at one-fiftieth of that cost. All results are SenseTime's own, and the GitHub roadmap still lists the 27B weights as pending. (Theme: new AI models and tools)

# ASIA

### Korea and Japan hold their second AI working-level talks
More than 30 officials from the two governments, including both countries' AI Safety Institutes, met at South Korea's Foreign Ministry on 21 October to share AI policy updates and explore "full-stack" AI cooperation and joint research, with specific talks on AI safety governance, standards, intellectual property and coordination in multilateral forums. They plan to hold the meetings regularly. No agreements were announced. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

# INDIA

### Principal Scientific Adviser says India is open to a dedicated AI law
Ajay Kumar Sood told NDTV the government is open to a dedicated AI law and has begun preparing a consultation paper on "targeted regulatory guardrails", saying AI risks have become much clearer in the last year or two. He singled out agentic AI as a "new challenge" and pointed to CERT-In's advice that agents run in sandboxes with a human approving critical actions. No draft or timeline has been published. (Theme: AI policy and regulation)

Source: NDTV https://www.ndtv.com/artificial-intelligence/india-to-bring-new-ai-law-what-top-government-adviser-ajay-kumar-sood-told-ndtv-12214525

OpenAI and Karmayogi Bharat signed an MoU on 21 October to put approved OpenAI Academy courses on the iGOT Karmayogi platform, which has more than 1.7 crore registered government employees, covering eligible staff across central and state governments and Union territories. OpenAI provides the content; Karmayogi Bharat handles access and coordination. (Theme: AI industry and startup news)
