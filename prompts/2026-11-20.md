### NextDC vows to keep future datacentres away from homes after community backlash in Melbourne
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/nov/20/nextdc-vows-to-keep-future-datacentres-away-from-homes-after-community-backlash-in-melbourne

NextDC says it will stop pursuing new data centre developments close to homes, after community opposition in inner Melbourne cut its share price by 40 percent, according to the Guardian. Chief executive Craig Scroggie said public expectations of data centres have shifted in the era of artificial intelligence, and the company will change how it deals with local communities. ### California investigates Nvidia over whether chip exports ended up in China
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/20/california-investigates-nvidia-over-whether-chip-exports-ended-up-in-china

California has issued an investigative subpoena to Nvidia, the Guardian reports, following a scandal that led co-founder and longtime chief executive Jensen Huang to step down. State Attorney General Rob Bonta is examining whether the California-based company's advanced semiconductor chips were exported in ways that ended up in China, in breach of US restrictions. Bonta said the conduct of Nvidia and its former chief raises serious warning signs, including over how the company may have misled investors. He described Nvidia's position as a complicated, cross-border situation, but stressed that under corporate securities law, "companies cannot mislead their investors." The subpoena was issued on Thursday. It came a week after Huang resigned, when it emerged he had had multiple affairs, one of them with Alice Huang, who is suspected of being a Chinese spy.

### Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26444

Researchers have unveiled a new testing tool for robots, in a paper titled Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation. The goal of so-called embodied intelligence is robots that handle complex physical jobs as flexibly and dependably as people. That demands three skills: reasoning based on past events, carrying out movements accurately and on time, and combining the two using feedback from the surroundings. Existing tests, the researchers say, cannot pinpoint which of these skills caused a failure. Their benchmark, a diagnostic test suite, offers 30 simulated object-handling tasks drawn from five real-world application areas.

### Traumatized teens sue school, cops after Doritos bag triggers AI gun alert
Lead: yes
Source: Ars Technica
https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/traumatized-teens-sue-school-cops-after-doritos-bag-triggers-ai-gun-alert/

Four Black high school students in Baltimore County are suing their school and police after an AI gun detection system mistook a bag of chips for a weapon, Ars Technica reports. In October 2025, the teens were sharing Cool Ranch Doritos after football practice while waiting to be picked up. The school's AI system then flagged one of them, who was holding the snack bag, as a possible gunman. Baltimore County Public Schools officials reviewed the linked security camera footage and, within two minutes, concluded there was no real threat. Recognising the Doritos bag, they cancelled the alert, which told other school staff and the Baltimore County Police Department that no response was required.

### Lagarde says EU funds should take first losses on chip plants, data centers
Source: CNBC
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/lagarde-eu-data-center-funding.html

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde said on Friday that public money from the European Union should absorb the first losses on large chip-plant and data-center projects, as a way to draw in private investment, CNBC reports. Speaking at the Frankfurt European Banking Congress, in her last address there before leaving the ECB in February, Lagarde said that where a challenge needs a European answer, the institutions able to deliver it should be reinforced. In technology, she argued, projects requiring European scale call for public-private partnerships at the European level, with public funds, including the EU budget, "taking the first losses and drawing in private capital." Lagarde has warned before that Europe lacks the data center capacity to meet its own needs, and that current plans for up to seven gigafactories across the European Union fall short.

# ASIA

### Anthropic's Australian data centre overwhelmingly opposed by residents
Source: The Economic Times
https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/anthropics-australian-data-centre-overwhelmingly-opposed-by-residents/articleshow/136010716.cms

Residents in regional Australia are overwhelmingly against a planned data centre that would have Anthropic as its anchor tenant, according to a project filing reported by The Economic Times. The proposed Western Downs Digital Park sits about 250 kilometres, or 155 miles, west of Brisbane and would be the AI company's first Australian facility, setting up a confrontation with the local community. Of nearly 600 public submissions, only two backed the project and one was neutral. The other 595 either opposed it or sought a delay until developers resolved certain environmental issues. The filing, lodged on behalf of developer Zerra DC, acknowledged that many submissions raised reasonable concerns about water, power, noise, roads, housing and the size of the project. Zerra and Anthropic representatives could not immediately be reached for comment.
