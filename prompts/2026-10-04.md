# WORLD

### Canada launches a national AI council
Source: Prime Minister of Canada
https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/10/02/prime-minister-carney-launches-new-national-council-artificial
Desk: ChatGPT and Grok

The Prime Minister of Canada said the government has established a National Council on Artificial Intelligence to advise on AI adoption, sovereign infrastructure, safety, economic competitiveness, and democratic resilience. The 13-member body includes AI pioneer Yoshua Bengio, Mila chief executive Valérie Pisano, Cloudflare co-founder Michelle Zatlyn, and other industry leaders.

### Firmus faces questions over a multibillion-dollar listing
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/oct/03/ai-datacentre-backlash-firmus-asx-float-tasmania-australia
Desk: Scraper

The Guardian reported that Firmus Technologies is preparing an Australian listing aimed at raising 7 billion dollars, which would be among the largest floats in the country's history. Residents in Launceston said construction of an AI data center was underway before many neighbours understood the scale of the plan. Investors quoted by the paper called the company's forecasts difficult to accept.

# USA

### Senators introduce liability rules for AI agents
Source: U.S. Senate Press Release
https://www.murphy.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/murphy-hawley-announce-breakthrough-bipartisan-legislation-to-force-ai-developers-to-prioritize-safety-or-face-prison-time
Desk: ChatGPT and Grok

Senators Chris Murphy and Josh Hawley introduced a bill that would create civil and criminal liability for AI developers and operators when autonomous agents cause unauthorized computer intrusions or cyber-related damage. The Senate press release says the measure is meant to require developers to prioritize safety.

### Meta reports mathematical results with Muse Spark
Source: Meta AI Research
https://research.meta.ai/blog/solving-open-research-problems-together
Desk: ChatGPT and Grok

Meta published six research papers describing mathematicians working with Muse Spark models on open problems in probability, optimization, algebra, and differential equations. The company said the work kept human oversight, independent review, and clear attribution.

### Meta opens the Muse Gadgets hardware platform
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/meta-wants-you-to-build-your-own-muse-gadget/
Desk: ChatGPT and Grok

TechCrunch reported that Meta has released Muse Gadgets, an open-source hardware project that lets developers connect Muse to Raspberry Pi boards, sensors, displays, smart-home gear, and other embedded devices through ESP32 firmware and a Linux software kit.

### OpenAI safety employee resigns
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/03/openai-safety-employee-resigns-claiming-the-companys-culture-is-broken/
Desk: Scraper

TechCrunch reported that David Robinson, who wrote the safety reports that accompanied OpenAI's major model releases, has resigned. He said the company's culture is broken, and he is making that case in The Atlantic.

### Georgia reviews whether AI can expose secret ballots
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/02/midterms-ai-ballot-privacy
Desk: Scraper

The Guardian reported that Georgia called an emergency meeting after a Princeton researcher showed that public election records could be combined with an AI model to link voters to ballots. Max Springer, of Princeton's Center for Information Technology Policy, said a low-cost model and records obtained under the state's open-records law produced a pipeline that could identify secret ballots, and that the system described what further data it would need to name real voters.

### OpenAI's Dot agent reaches into everyday tasks
Source: The Verge
https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004096/openai-chatgpt-dots-hands-on-agent
Desk: Scraper

The Verge reported that OpenAI's Dot agent is built like workplace software that can also handle ordinary errands, such as ordering dinner. Each person gets one Dot for now, with a customizable avatar. The work appears in a second window beside the chat.

### Anthropic warns that government sales carry extra risk
Source: The Hindu
https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/technology/anthropic-warns-government-attitudes-may-hurt-customer-ties-ipo-prospectus-shows/article71539554.ece
Desk: Scraper

The Hindu reported that Anthropic's listing prospectus warns government attitudes toward the company or its technology could hurt customer relationships. The company said selling to government agencies brings risks of its own, including a change in how those agencies view Anthropic.

### Amazon urges communities not to block data centers
Source: The Verge
https://www.theverge.com/tech/1003929/amazon-ai-data-center-blog-warning
Desk: Scraper

The Verge reported that Amazon Web Services chief executive Matt Garman published a long argument asking communities to support AI data center projects. He said blocking them would harm the U.S. economy and national security, and he disputed claims about jobs, power use, and the environment.

### The White House renames AI as super intelligence
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/video/its-not-ai-anymore-its-super-intelligence-according-to-the-white-house/
Desk: Scraper

TechCrunch reported that the White House gathered chief executives including Mark Zuckerberg, Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, and Anthropic's Dario Amodei to sign an AI safety pledge the president called morally binding. The same report said an executive order renames artificial intelligence as super intelligence.

### Apple tightens full-disk access on Macs
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/
Desk: Scraper

TechCrunch reported that Apple will add controls around the macOS Full Disk Access permission. Apple said more capable AI agents make broad access to files, messages, mail, and browsing history riskier.

### Banks look for people who can direct AI agents
Source: CNBC
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/02/ai-redefining-wall-street-jobs.html
Desk: Scraper

CNBC reported that banks are hiring AI engineers who can coordinate teams of specialized agents, a skill it said is now in sharp demand as Wall Street rewrites job descriptions around the technology.

### Restaurant staff say chatbot answers are complicating allergies
Source: The Verge
https://www.theverge.com/report/1002963/ai-hallucinations-customer-service-jobs-agents
Desk: Scraper

The Verge reported that restaurant workers are seeing guests rely on chatbot answers when a dish contains an allergen. A New York server said diners have ordered food made with shellfish after being told they have a shellfish allergy, then argued with the staff because a chatbot disagreed.

# ASIA

### Tokyo Electric moves against unused AI power reservations
Source: Nikkei Asia
https://asia.nikkei.com/business/energy/japan-s-tepco-to-counter-capacity-squatters-in-ai-data-center-boom
Desk: ChatGPT and Grok

Nikkei Asia reported that Tokyo Electric Power is tightening how it allocates electricity to AI data centers. The utility wants to stop projects from reserving grid capacity and then failing to build. Power, not only chips, is now the constraint.

### Karnataka picks seven deeptech startups for scale-up support
Source: The Economic Times
https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/karnataka-govts-innoverse-identifies-seven-startups-for-deeptech-scale-up-programme/articleshow/134641803.cms

The Economic Times reported that Karnataka's Innoverse programme has selected seven startups for its first Deeptech Enabler cohort, covering AI, autonomy, robotics, and defense-related work. The state wants the group to commercialize faster and to draw more than 300 crore rupees in growth capital.
