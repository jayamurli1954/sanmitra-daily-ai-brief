# WORLD

### AllocFlow: End-to-End Portfolio Trading Action Generation from Raw Market Data
Desk: Research Watch
Source: arXiv Pre-print
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19937

Managing an investment portfolio means turning messy market data into decisions about many assets at once, all drawing on the same limited pool of money. In a paper titled AllocFlow: End-to-End Portfolio Trading Action Generation from Raw Market Data, the researchers argue that conventional systems split prediction and portfolio building into separate steps, which can pull learning away from the real goals of allocating and trading. Their system, AllocFlow, uses a generative technique called flow matching to produce allocations for every asset together, straight from raw price histories. A Transformer model that looks across assets lets it set long or short position sizes jointly.

https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6wy4xv2mxglo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

### US government confirms investigation into tech firm Megaspeed
Lead: yes
Source: BBC
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmn504nq5033o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

American authorities have confirmed an investigation into Singapore-based computing firm Megaspeed, which they suspect of breaching US export controls by helping move Nvidia chips to China, the BBC reports. The Department of Justice and the Department of Commerce said in a joint statement that the inquiry began in 2024. The confirmation follows questions over whether advanced AI chips reached China in spite of American restrictions. Those concerns grew in recent weeks after Nvidia founder Jensen Huang disclosed an extramarital affair, reportedly with former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang. US officials suspect her of spying for Beijing, a claim China rejects.

### Baidu’s Robin Li warns of AI bubble risk as applications lag infrastructure spending
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/17/baidus-robin-li-warns-of-ai-bubble-risk-as-applications-lag-infrastructure-spending/

Baidu chief executive Robin Li cautioned on Tuesday that artificial intelligence could become a bubble unless companies convert their use of the technology into stronger profits, TechNode reports. Addressing the APEC CEO Summit in Shenzhen, Li said AI applications have spread more slowly than hoped, even as infrastructure spending, model development and token consumption have all surged. The remarks extended a theme he raised at Baidu World last week about organizing companies around AI.
