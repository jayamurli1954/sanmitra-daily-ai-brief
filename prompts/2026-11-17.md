# CHINA

### US government confirms investigation into tech firm Megaspeed
Lead: yes
Source: BBC
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmn504nq5033o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

The United States has confirmed it is investigating computing company Megaspeed, suspecting it of violating US export controls by helping to ship tech giant Nvidia's chips to China. In a joint statement, the US Department of Justice and Department of Commerce said they had been investigating Singapore-based firm Megaspeed since 2024. It comes after questions were raised about the possibility high-end AI chips may have ended up in China despite US export restrictions. Concerns emerged in recent weeks after Nvidia founder Jensen Huang revealed he had an extramarital affair , allegedly with former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang. US authorities suspect her of spying for Beijing, which China denies.

### Baidu’s Robin Li warns of AI bubble risk as applications lag infrastructure spending
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/17/baidus-robin-li-warns-of-ai-bubble-risk-as-applications-lag-infrastructure-spending/

Baidu CEO Robin Li warned on Tuesday that the AI industry could face a bubble if businesses fail to turn adoption into higher profits. Speaking at the APEC CEO Summit in Shenzhen, Li said the spread of AI applications had fallen short of expectations despite heavy spending on infrastructure, rapid model development and rising token use. Li said companies need to rethink how they organize work to turn AI capabilities into business gains. He pointed to changes Baidu has made internally, building on a theme he raised at Baidu World last week about how companies can organize around AI.

# USA

### OpenAI sued by California over Hugging Face attack
Source: BBC
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6wy4xv2mxglo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI on Monday, alleging the ChatGPT-maker misled and endangered the public by developing artificial intelligence (AI) models without proper safeguards. In the lawsuit, Bonta cited the July 2026 attack on the AI platform Hugging Face by experimental OpenAI agents which accessed the internet to breach that company's internal systems. Bonta claimed OpenAI created a public nuisance and violated the state's unfair competition laws. OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri told the BBC in a statement that Bonta's lawsuit "appears to single out OpenAI over a challenge that's been identified by every major AI lab".
