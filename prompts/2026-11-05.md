### Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3.1 Pro model weights
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/05/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-1-pro-model-weights/

Moonshot AI has released the weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro on Hugging Face, making its flagship multimodal model available for independent deployment. The model uses a mixture-of-experts architecture with 2.8 trillion total parameters and approximately 104 billion activated per inference, and supports a context window of around one million tokens. K3.1 Pro builds on Kimi K3 with expanded reinforcement learning for coding, 3D creation, financial research and long-running agent tasks. The weights release follows the model’s earlier availability through Kimi’s applications and API. Huawei has also announced inference support through vLLM Ascend and SGLang, covering its Atlas A3 and Ascend 950 systems.

### Nothing plans December launch for first AI-native device
Source: TechNode
https://technode.com/2026/11/05/nothing-plans-december-launch-for-first-ai-native-device/

Nothing plans to launch its first AI-native hardware product in December, founder and CEO Carl Pei said on November 4. Pei described the forthcoming device as a productivity tool without a screen, adding that its functions could not have been delivered through an app alone. In an essay titled “Toolmakers,” Pei argued that AI tools lack much of the context surrounding people’s work, including conversations and ideas that users do not manually enter into a chatbot. He suggested that everyday hardware could help close that gap. Nothing has not disclosed the device’s precise form, specifications or price.
