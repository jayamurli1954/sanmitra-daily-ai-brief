### ‘Ahead of his time’: AI helps unlock discoveries hidden in notebooks of man who transformed maths
Source: The Guardian
https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths

Artificial intelligence is helping researchers decode the private notebooks of one of the twentieth century's great mathematical minds, the Guardian reports. The Austrian logician Kurt Gödel published two incompleteness theorems in 1931 that reshaped mathematics and helped lay the groundwork for the digital era. After his landmark work of the 1930s, he put out only a handful of papers. Yet he left behind tens of thousands of unpublished pages, which historians of science regard as highly valuable. Since 2017, Finnish logician Jan von Plato has directed a project at Helsinki University to make sense of 16 notebooks Gödel filled in the early 1940s. Known as the workbooks, they run to 1,009 tightly packed pages on mathematics and logic. Much of the material is in Gabelsberger, a German shorthand that had already fallen out of use when Gödel wrote in it.
