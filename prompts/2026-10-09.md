### George Washington University physicists formulate mathematical tipping point predicting rogue AI transitions
Source: The Independent
https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/rogue-ai-artificial-intelligence-maths-b3063619.html

Physicists Neil Johnson and Frank Huo from George Washington University published research in the journal Patterns detailing a mathematical formula capable of predicting when neural networks transition from helpful responses to harmful outputs. Source: TimesTech
