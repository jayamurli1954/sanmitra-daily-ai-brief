# WORLD

### OpenAI uses its models to improve semiconductor chip design
Lead: yes
Source: OpenAI
https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/

OpenAI says its own artificial intelligence models are now helping engineers refine computer chips. That includes Jalapeño, the company's custom chip built for inference, the work of running trained models, as well as hardware from outside vendors. In one example the company cited, a redesigned multiplier circuit took up roughly 15% less cell area and cut latency from two clock cycles down to one. OpenAI added that other design gains have already been proven in physical silicon. Separately, it is working with Synopsys to connect frontier models with electronic design automation software. The company suggests the approach could speed up chip engineering and make computing more efficient.

### Southeast Asia’s data center boom leaned on Chinese demand. New U.S. rules put that pipeline at risk
Source: CNBC
https://is.gd/StwLlP

New American export controls could slow Southeast Asia's fast-growing data center construction, CNBC reports. An interim rule issued by the Commerce Department on Monday requires facilities located in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia to secure a U.S. government license before purchasing advanced artificial intelligence chips, including those made by Nvidia. Operators that already serve major Chinese customers have 90 days to apply, or they could face export restrictions later on. The rule targets the region's expanding role as a provider of computing capacity to Chinese firms. Data center building across Southeast Asia has surged, fueled by demand for the chips used to train and develop AI models. Because American restrictions already block Nvidia's most advanced processors from reaching China, Chinese technology companies including Alibaba Group and ByteDance have been renting capacity in overseas facilities instead.

### TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks
Desk: Research of the Week
Source: arXiv
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22115

Researchers have unveiled Xing 4, a new family of large language models that builds on the earlier TeleChat series, in a paper titled TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks. The problem they tackle is that most open-source AI models are trained on GPUs, and scaling up on domestic hardware and software, here Ascend processors and the MindSpore framework, raises tough engineering challenges. The paper explains how the team chose the model's design and handled large-scale pretraining, along with follow-up training for reasoning and for agentic work, where the model carries out tasks on its own. They weighed model capacity against costs such as computation and memory access, using a mixture-of-experts design, in which only part of the model switches on for each token, and speculative decoding, where a small draft model proposes text ahead of the main model to speed up output. They have released a 29-billion-parameter model trained entirely on Ascend NPUs that uses just 4 billion parameters per token and natively handles contexts of 256K tokens. Their infrastructure also supported pretraining a 285-billion-parameter model on 8,192 Ascend 910C cards. Measurements showed up to 4.68 times faster processing of input prompts than a baseline, 2.67 to 3.77 times faster text generation from speculative decoding, and a 16.1% throughput gain from dropping pipeline parallelism. For post-training, they built TeleGym, a platform linking agent execution, evaluation and learning. The researchers say training of a trillion-parameter model is under way.

# USA

### Lagarde says EU funds should take first losses on chip plants, data centers
Source: CNBC
https://is.gd/1vmlMJ

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde said on Friday that European Union public funds should shoulder the initial losses on major chip factory and data center projects in order to attract private investors, according to CNBC. Addressing the Frankfurt European Banking Congress for the final time before she departs the ECB in February, Lagarde argued that when a problem demands a European response, the bodies capable of providing it should be strengthened. On technology, she said ventures needing continental scale require partnerships between the public and private sectors, organised Europe-wide, with public money, including the EU budget, "taking the first losses and drawing in private capital." Lagarde has previously cautioned that Europe does not have enough data center capacity for its own requirements, and that existing plans for as many as seven gigafactories in the bloc are insufficient.

### Soket AI launches EKA-1 foundation model with 4 million dollar backing
Source: Analytics India Magazine
https://is.gd/rAMgfA

Soket AI has launched EKA-1, an open foundation model built for mathematical reasoning, coding and logic tasks in more than 100 languages from India and the Global South, Analytics India Magazine reports. The 26-billion parameter model is trained to capture regional linguistic subtleties while remaining competitive with leading global frontier models. The launch is backed by a four million dollar seed round, with Bajaj Finserv and Pennant Technologies leading, and is expected to speed up homegrown development of multilingual AI infrastructure in India.
