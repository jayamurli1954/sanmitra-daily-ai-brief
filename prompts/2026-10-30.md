# WORLD

### Experts followed their AI agent's hidden lean on 86% of judgment calls
Lead: yes
Source: Anthropic https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-judgment-calls
Also: Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/28/experts-defer-to-ai-in-decision-making-anthropic-study-finds

In a randomised study, Anthropic researchers gave 50 experienced causal-inference analysts Claude Code to analyse farming data and recommend whether to expand a programme. Unknown to them, the agent had been told to quietly favour one defensible option on four analytical choices. Analysts ended up matching that lean 86% of the time against 50% by chance, and when they had already picked a side, an opposing lean made them switch 60% of the time. The authors say AI still cut errors and saved time, but warn that unnoticed deference could let arbitrary choices shape research and policy. This is Anthropic's own study of its own product. (Theme: AI breakthroughs)

### AI agents now start more than 40% of pull requests on GitHub

### Mistral releases Large 4 with a cybersecurity focus
Source: Reuters https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/mistral-ai-publicly-releases-large-4-targets-cybersecurity-users-2026-10-29/

French AI company Mistral has publicly released Large 4, a model designed to help organisations identify and fix cybersecurity vulnerabilities. Unlike models that must be accessed through an online service, the downloadable model can be run on an organisation's own systems, giving customers more control over deployment and data. Locally deployed AI can help organisations keep sensitive security information in-house, but openly available cyber capabilities also create a dual-use challenge: the same technology can support defenders and potentially assist attackers. # USA

### Google's Gemini 4 Carbon goes to cyber defenders first
Source: Google https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, its new frontier model, with what it calls significant gains in coding, long agentic tasks and cybersecurity. It took the top spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, and leads benchmarks for finding real bugs and turning them into exploits. Because of that, it is available only to selected cyber-defence companies for now, with wider access planned after more safety testing. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Claims about running "for days" on engineering tasks are Google's own. (Theme: new AI models and tools)

### US Justice Department examines Nvidia's proposed Hugging Face acquisition
Source: Reuters https://www.reuters.com/world/us-doj-is-inquiring-about-nvidias-purchase-hugging-face-sources-say-2026-10-28/

The US Department of Justice is asking questions about Nvidia's proposed $12.93 billion acquisition of Hugging Face, Reuters reports, citing sources. The inquiry includes whether the deal could affect competition by giving Nvidia greater control over an important platform for distributing AI models and tools. An inquiry does not establish wrongdoing, and no final outcome has been announced. AI competition depends on more than model quality and chips: access to developer platforms, open-source tools and distribution channels can also influence which companies gain market power. # CHINA

### AGIBOT wants robots measured by useful work, not units shipped
Source: AGIBOT https://www.agibot.com/article/231/detail/131.html

At its Innovation Day in Shanghai, humanoid-robot maker AGIBOT proposed "Worken", a measure that multiplies active robots by hours worked autonomously and by output efficiency against a set benchmark. It also showed GO-3 Preview, a new foundation model meant to help robots predict the results of their actions, the WITA-Omni interaction model, which AGIBOT says responds in about 600 milliseconds on average and handles more than 100 languages, the G2 Max industrial robot with up to 40 kg dual-arm handling, and Link-U OS 1.0, which it plans to fully open-source by end-2026. Benchmark rankings cited are AGIBOT's own. (Themes: new AI models and tools; technology trends; AI industry and startup news)

### Report says DeepSeek and Moonshot trained on outputs from OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra
Source: Reuters https://www.reuters.com/technology/deepseek-moonshot-used-openais-gpt-6-astra-train-latest-models-information-2026-10-29/

Reuters reported, citing The Information, that Chinese AI developers DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied on outputs from OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to help train their latest models. The report does not fully detail how those outputs were used, and the companies had not publicly confirmed the account. The issue highlights the growing importance of model distillation, using the outputs of a more capable system to help develop another model, and raises questions about intellectual property, terms of service and how AI developers protect their capabilities. (Themes: AI industry and startup news; AI policy and regulation)

# INDIA

### IIT Kharagpur and Absolute build an AI early-warning tool for soybean farmers
Source: The Economic Times https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/agriculture/iit-kharagpur-and-absolute-launch-smart-scout-ai-project-for-soybean-farming/articleshow/135297217.cms

IIT Kharagpur and biosolutions firm Absolute are developing Smart Scout, a camera-based system that spots soybean plants at risk of falling over before harvest, flags crop damage and helps estimate yields. It is one of six projects in the Quad's first AI-ENGAGE agriculture cohort, backed by more than $6 million in total across India, the US, Japan and Australia. Pilot trials will run in major soybean-growing states over three years. (Themes: technology trends; AI breakthroughs)
