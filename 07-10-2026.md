### OpenAI introduces textGrain invisible watermarks across ChatGPT outputs in the European Union
Source: TechCrunch
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/openai-will-start-watermarking-chatgpts-text-in-the-eu/
Also: The Verge https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004880/openai-chatgpt-text-watermarks-eu-ai-act

OpenAI announced the rollout of textGrain, an invisible cryptographic watermarking system applied to text generated by ChatGPT and Codex for users in the European Union to comply with transparency provisions under the EU AI Act. The watermarking embeds statistical token patterns into generated prose that resist copy-paste extraction while remaining imperceptible to human readers. While detection accuracy reaches ninety-two percent on unmodified text, OpenAI cautioned that heavy synonym substitution and translation reduce detection efficacy, noting that absent watermarks cannot conclusively establish human authorship.
